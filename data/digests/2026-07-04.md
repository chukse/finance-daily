## What Moved Markets

**Rotation, not rally.** The S&P 500 was flat on the day but the internals were sharply divided. The Dow +1.14% was lifted by large-cap value names while the Nasdaq -0.80% and QQQ -1.73% dragged on tech selling.

**Yields & dollar stable.** 10Y at 4.37%, DXY at 100.86 — neither a catalyst nor a headwind today.

**Gold +1.81% to $4,187** is the standout macro move. With Khamenei's funeral underway in Tehran, China launching coast guard patrols east of Taiwan, and a Ukrainian drone strike hitting a St. Petersburg oil terminal, safe-haven demand had multiple drivers. WTI barely reacted, +0.13% to $68.78, suggesting the geopolitical premium in oil remains capped by supply.

**Bitcoin flat** at $62,555; ETH +0.3%. Crypto offered no signal today.
