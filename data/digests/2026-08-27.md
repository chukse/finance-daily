# Daily Market Brief — 2026-08-27

**TL;DR: Nvidia's blowout earnings added $400B+ in market cap and ignited a broad tech rally, with Jackson Hole's Warsh speech the next major catalyst.**

---

## What Moved Markets

**Nvidia / AI trade** was the session's dominant force. NVDA surged +8.74% after a blowout revenue forecast, erasing nearly $300–400B+ in market cap losses and dragging Nasdaq up +1.57% vs. the S&P's more modest +0.72% and Dow's +0.20% — a clear sign the move was tech-specific.

**Software coattails were dramatic.** Salesforce +22% (second-best day ever), CrowdStrike (best day ever), and Okta +~29% all surged as AI-driven demand expectations were repriced higher alongside Nvidia's results.

**VIX dropped -4.60% to 14.51**, reflecting the risk-on tone. The 10Y yield edged up +1.7 bps to 4.672% — markets absorbing supply after a 7-year auction priced near 2-year highs without notable disruption.

**Commodities caught a bid across the board.** WTI +1.61% to $83.55 as Russia-Ukraine tensions intensified and Iran war news continued. Wheat hit a 3-year high on Black Sea crisis deterioration. Gold +1.22% to $4,654 as a pre-Warsh haven bid persisted.

**Jackson Hole looms.** Three Fed officials issued inflation warnings ahead of Chair Warsh's Friday speech. Fed's Collins explicitly said a rate *increase* is warranted if inflation disappoints — that's the hawkish tail risk markets are watching.
