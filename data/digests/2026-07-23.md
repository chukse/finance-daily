# Daily Market Brief — 2026-07-23

**TL;DR:** Oil's 4.7% surge toward $91 (Houthis targeting Saudi tankers, Kazakhstan supply disrupted) drove inflation fears, pushed 10Y yields to 4.71% — the highest since Jan 2025 — and dragged risk assets broadly lower while Big Tech earnings disappointed on spending.

---

## What Moved Markets

**Oil & inflation shock** dominated. WTI crude jumped $4.12 to $90.94, with MarketWatch flagging $100 as a near-term target after Houthis claimed strikes on Saudi tankers and drone attacks on Kazakhstan's export terminal sharply cut output. Airlines were immediate casualties: American Airlines slashed its 2026 earnings outlook on spiking fuel costs; Southwest's Q3 forecast missed for the same reason.

**Rates repriced higher.** The 10Y Treasury yield climbed 5 bps to 4.707%, its highest since January 2025, compounding equity pressure. The VIX spiked 13.6% to 18.91 — the sharpest single-day vol move in weeks — signaling genuine anxiety rather than routine drift.

**Big Tech earnings rekindled AI spending concerns.** Alphabet fell after disclosing a larger-than-expected capex increase; Google also absorbed a $1B EU fine under the Digital Markets Act. Reuters flagged the cash burn issue as an industry-wide concern. MSFT dropped 1.86%, AMZN -1.09%, GOOGL -1.46%.

**Small caps led losses.** Russell 2000 fell 0.92% — most exposed to rate sensitivity and domestic fuel cost pass-through.

**Gold sold off 1.95% to $4,066** despite the risk-off tone, consistent with dollar strength (+0.24%) and real yields moving higher. Bitcoin -1.60%, ETH -1.80%.
