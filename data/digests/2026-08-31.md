# Daily Market Brief — 2026-08-31

**Daily Brief — August 31, 2026**

**TL;DR:** U.S. strikes on Iran sent oil surging 2.6% and spiked mortgage rates, dragging equities lower across the board to close out an otherwise winning August.

---

## What Moved Markets

**The macro shock:** U.S. military strikes on Iran drove WTI crude up $2.13 to $85.53, the highest in months. Mortgage rates jumped to their highest since June 2025 on the oil move and associated inflation fears. The 10Y yield climbed 3.6 bps to 4.756%, with MarketWatch flagging 5% as a credible next target — driven less by Fed policy and more by fiscal concerns after Bessent signaled a debt-reduction plan is still months away.

**Equities:** Broad selloff, though relatively contained. Small-caps led losses — Russell 2000 -0.86% — consistent with rate sensitivity. The VIX popped 4.2% to 15.0, still low in absolute terms. Despite today's drop, Wall Street ended August in positive territory.

**Dollar/Gold:** Dollar slipped 0.29% to 99.41 — unusual given a risk-off geopolitical event; may reflect fiscal credibility concerns. Gold added 0.2% to $4,486; Comex gold finished the month up 9.43%.

**Crypto:** Modest bid with risk-off elsewhere — BTC +1.21% to $78,772, ETH +1.12% — crypto acting as a partial safe haven rather than a risk proxy today.
