# Daily Market Brief — 2026-11-05

**TL;DR:** A Democratic midterm sweep triggered a brutal AI/tech rout — Nasdaq down 5%, VIX up 58% — on fears of regulatory headwinds, data-center scrutiny, and tighter fiscal policy.

---

## What Moved Markets

**The catalyst:** Democrats swept the midterms, shifting the political calculus on AI infrastructure spending, crypto regulation, and big-tech oversight. Markets repriced fast and hard.

**Equities:** S&P 500 −3.90% (7,389), Nasdaq −5.05% (24,436), Dow −3.01% (51,752), Russell 2000 −4.32%. The post-election rally from 2024 is fully erased. Nasdaq is in confirmed correction territory.

**Volatility:** VIX surged 58% to 26.3 — the largest single-day spike since April. Traders are publicly saying even that understates realized fear.

**Rates:** 10Y Treasury yield fell 1.33% to 4.83% — a flight-to-quality bid. Bank of England hiked rates for the first time since 2023, flagging inflation approaching 5%, adding to global tightening anxiety. Fed's Hammack and Barkin both signaled a gradual rate-hike path remains live.

**Oil & Gold:** WTI crude +2.41% to $90.24 (energy seen as less exposed to new Democratic regulatory push). Gold +0.97% to $4,112 as a haven trade.

**Crypto:** Bitcoin −5.37% to $76,087, Ethereum −7.04% to $2,252. A Democratic Washington is seen as hostile to crypto deregulation hopes.
