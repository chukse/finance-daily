# Daily Market Brief — 2026-08-24

**Daily Brief — August 24, 2026**

**TL;DR:** Broad equity gains mask rising tension — gold hit $4,715 (+2.0%), the VIX jumped 4.9%, and 10Y yields climbed to 4.74% as U.S.-Iran sanctions and a Canada trade breakdown rattled risk sentiment beneath the surface.

## What Moved Markets

**Equities up, but defensively bid.** S&P 500 +0.43%, Dow +0.98%, Russell 2000 +0.85%. Small-caps and cyclicals outperformed large-cap tech, suggesting rotation rather than broad risk-on appetite.

**Gold surged $91 to $4,715.** The primary driver: the U.S. announced a "greatest financial offensive" against Iran while Tehran threatened seizure of 45 tankers in Hormuz. Geopolitical risk premium spiked hard.

**Oil fell 2.3% to $85.08** despite the Iran escalation — Hormuz flow data is contested (WSJ: trackers can't confirm oil is actually being rerouted), and demand concerns are weighing.

**10Y yield at 4.74% (+0.89%).** Treasury supply concerns are live — Bessent is reportedly weighing use of the ~$1T Treasury General Account to fund bond buybacks, an unusual signal of fiscal stress management. VIX +4.9% to 15.87 confirms unease.

**Canada trade collapse hit steel.** Ottawa-Washington talks broke down; Nucor and Steel Dynamics rallied on tariff protection expectations. Canadian dollar slid.

**Dollar soft at 98.97** despite yield rise — debt nervousness is undermining conventional USD support.
