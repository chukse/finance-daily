# Daily Market Brief — 2026-07-16

**TL;DR:** Mega-cap tech surged on strong earnings and analyst upgrades while Iran-Hormuz tensions lifted oil above $80 and a VIX spike signaled underlying unease despite broad index gains.

---

## What Moved Markets

**Equities broadly higher, led by tech.** S&P 500 +0.38% to 7,572, Nasdaq +0.62% to 26,269. Russell 2000 kept pace (+0.39%), suggesting the rally had some breadth.

**Healthcare: UnitedHealth the standout earner.** UNH blew past Q2 estimates and raised full-year guidance as cost controls took hold — the clearest positive earnings catalyst of the session.

**Rates and the dollar inched higher.** 10Y yield +5 bps to 4.59%, putting quiet pressure on duration. Dollar Index barely moved (+0.13%), keeping no meaningful FX headwind on multinationals.

**Oil +1.1% to $80.46** on Iran's explicit warning that Hormuz is a "red line" amid ongoing U.S. strike threats and active airstrikes. Red Sea route risk adds a second supply-disruption vector.

**Gold -0.97% to $4,005** — profit-taking or mild risk-on rotation; still holding the $4,000 level.

**VIX +4.2% to 16.3** despite green tape — options market is hedging geopolitical tail risk even as indices rally.

**Retail sales beat** (car buyers + Amazon Prime Day cited), reinforcing the "economy hasn't lost its mojo" narrative and supporting the risk-on tone.

**New Brazil tariff: 25% on most goods** — U.S. escalates trade actions beyond Asia; limited immediate market reaction but a watch item for EM and agricultural flows.
