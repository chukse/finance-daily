# Daily Market Brief — 2026-10-23

**TL;DR:** Oil surges 3.4% on Middle East escalation and a UAE spill, pushing the 10Y yield through 5% and hammering the Dow 500+ points while tech held its ground.

## What Moved Markets

**Oil & Middle East:** WTI crude jumped $3.41 to $102.86, with Brent topping $116 — a six-month high — on rising regional tensions. A UAE coastal oil spill added supply-disruption optics. The US authorized an emergency 400,000-barrel SPR release, insufficient to offset the move.

**Rates:** The 10Y Treasury yield hit 5.00% (+0.93% on the day), a psychologically significant level that pressured rate-sensitive equities. The headline cited investors "seeking cover from AI worries" alongside the geopolitical bid for safety being outweighed by inflation concerns from oil.

**Equities — divergence:** The Dow shed 500+ points (-0.96%) dragged by energy-cost and consumer-sentiment pressure. The Nasdaq outperformed (+0.24%) as mega-cap tech partially offset broader weakness. VIX stayed contained at 19.0, suggesting no panic.

**Gold:** Fell 1.26% to $4,009 despite the risk-off tone — likely pressured by the stronger dollar (+0.15% to 101.76) and the 5% yield competing for safe-haven flows.

**Consumer Sentiment:** US consumer sentiment near a record low ahead of midterms, adding a macro headwind to the day's narrative.
