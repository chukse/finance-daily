# Daily Market Brief — 2026-08-05

**TL;DR:** Broad risk-on rally pushed the S&P 500 to new highs (+1.79%) on surprise jobs resilience and Middle East de-escalation hopes, while gold surged 3.87% on Hormuz uncertainty and Fed hawks rattled bonds.

---

## What moved markets

**Equities — broad advance, tech leads.** Nasdaq +2.59%, S&P 500 +1.79%, Russell 2000 +1.85% — essentially a clean sweep. The catalyst mix: better-than-feared labor data (ADP's 44K private payroll miss was offset by a "surprise" in the official jobs print per headlines), Mideast de-escalation hopes around Hormuz, and continued AI-spending momentum from Arista and Lilly earnings.

**Jobs — contradictory signals.** ADP July private payrolls came in at just 44,000 — weakest in six months — yet a separate jobs report apparently beat expectations enough to lift futures. Labor market picture is genuinely murky.

**Rates & Fed — hawkish pivot noise.** 10Y yield nudged up 4bp to 4.629%. Kashkari explicitly said "now is the time to start slowly moving rates up," and KC Fed's Schmid backed higher rates to curb inflation. The market shrugged but gold didn't.

**Gold +3.87% to $4,254** — the standout macro move. Hormuz negotiations, dollar softness (DXY -0.14% to 99.75), and stagflation hedging all cited. Silver also rising.

**Oil +0.22% to $75.94.** Houthis claimed a strike on a Saudi tanker in the Red Sea, providing a modest bid despite Hormuz optimism capping upside.

**VIX +2.18% to 16.86** — unusual: vol rose alongside equities, suggesting some hedging activity even as stocks hit highs. Michael Burry's 1987 comparison is circulating.
