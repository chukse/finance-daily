# Daily Brief — November 4, 2026

**TL;DR:** Democrats swept the 2026 midterms, flipping Congress and triggering a broad risk-on rally as markets priced in de-escalation of the Iran conflict and reduced tariff risk.

---

## What Moved Markets

**The election was the tape.** A Democratic congressional sweep drove the day's key cross-asset moves:

- **Equities surged.** S&P 500 +1.70%, Dow +2.89% (small-cap and value leading), Russell 2000 +1.57%. The Dow's outsized gain reflects rotation into industrials and financials.
- **VIX cratered 18.5%** to 16.98 — the market's fear gauge pricing out tail risk around Iran escalation and trade wars.
- **Oil crashed 8.0% to $88.42.** The dominant read: a Democratic Congress constrains Trump's Iran war authority and opens diplomatic off-ramps. WTI posted one of its sharpest single-day drops of the year.
- **Gold +3.4% to $4,073** — flight-to-safety bid unwinding from geopolitical risk while simultaneously catching a dollar-weakness bid. USD index slipped 0.29% to 101.57.
- **10Y yield fell to 4.90%** (-0.97%) as markets reassess the fiscal trajectory with a split government limiting deficit expansion.
- **Bitcoin +3.8% to $80,311; ETH +6.5%.** Crypto's midterm lobbying blitz failed — Democratic winners are generally more hostile to deregulation — but crypto caught the broader risk-on wave anyway.
- **Data center/AI infrastructure stocks slid** on Democratic wins: renewable energy beneficiaries rallied while permitting-dependent hyperscale buildout faces headwinds.
