# Daily Market Brief — 2026-08-29

**TL;DR:** Rising yields and a hawkish Fed posture hit small caps and crypto hard while mega-cap software names bucked the tape.

---

## What moved markets

**Rates & dollar drove the session.** The 10Y Treasury yield jumped 5 bps to 4.72% — near cycle highs — after economic data reinforced the hawkish Fed narrative telegraphed at Jackson Hole by Chair Warsh. The dollar index gained 0.54% to 99.70, adding pressure across risk assets.

**Small caps took the worst of it.** Russell 2000 fell 1.39%, the sharpest decline among major indices, as rate-sensitive smaller companies face disproportionate floating-rate debt exposure. The S&P 500 slipped 0.25%, Nasdaq 0.52%; the Dow was nearly flat (-0.02%).

**VIX eased 0.55% to 14.43** despite the selling — suggesting orderly repositioning rather than panic.

**Crypto sold off sharply.** Bitcoin dropped 3.0% to $77,846, Ethereum 2.64% to $2,444. The stronger dollar and higher real yields are the proximate drivers.

## Watchlist

**NVDA –4.57% to $217.55** — The dominant move. Earnings are imminent and WSJ flagged the print as a test of Wall Street's AI faith; traders appear to be de-risking ahead of the report rather than chasing.
