# Daily Market Brief — 2026-08-26

**TL;DR:** Markets edged higher ahead of tonight's NVDA earnings and tomorrow's PCE print, with a 10Y yield drop to 4.64% and crude sliding 2.3% doing the heavy lifting.

---

## What moved markets

**Risk-on but cautious.** S&P +0.32%, Nasdaq +0.66%, Russell +0.50% — broad but shallow gains. VIX ticked up 1.49% to 15.68 despite the green tape, signaling hedging ahead of two binary events: Nvidia Q2 earnings (after close) and July PCE data (Thursday).

**Rates provided the tailwind.** 10Y Treasury yield fell 138bps to 4.639%, its steepest single-day drop in weeks, even as Fed's Collins explicitly called for rates to rise "soon" absent proof inflation is still falling. The bond market is betting on a soft PCE print.

**Oil dropped hard.** WTI -2.29% to $80.47, despite WSJ headlines earlier this week about U.S. threatening economic measures that had briefly supported prices. Falling oil is calming inflation fears and acting as a consumer tax cut.

**Gold at $4,672.** Continued its climb (+0.75%), reflecting persistent dollar skepticism despite the DXY barely holding above 99.

## Stories worth knowing

**Intuit -12%.** TurboTax is losing customers at an accelerating rate due to price hikes. Revenue beat but unit losses are structural — users are migrating to free alternatives. Classic "good quarter, bad trajectory" sell-off.

**Boston Scientific cyberattack.** Shares falling intraday after confirming an active breach. No disclosed scope yet; med-device cybersecurity exposure back in focus.

**Zoom disappointed.** Q2 earnings and guidance underwhelmed; consumer segment slowdown cited. The post-pandemic normalization story has no bottom yet.

**Jackson Hole watch.** Markets are anxious for Kevin Warsh (presumed next Fed chair) to signal his policy framework. Any hawkish lean could reprice the entire rate-cut timeline heading into fall.

**Moody's Zandi "day of reckoning" warning.** Called current conditions — high debt, sticky rates, trade war drag — a converging set of crisis preconditions. Not a call, but worth tracking as a macro risk framing.
