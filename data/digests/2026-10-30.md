# Daily Market Brief — 2026-10-30

**TL;DR:** Tech wreckage from Intel's historic collapse and Amazon's capex-driven miss sent the Nasdaq down 2.94% and VIX up 21%, while war-driven oil and a Fed hawk piled on.

---

## What Moved Markets

**Intel catastrophe:** Intel suffered its worst single-session drop in over 50 years, wiping $12B off the U.S. government's stake and triggering a global chip rout. Analysts see no technical floor; the selloff cascaded into semis broadly — NVDA fell 4.57%.

**AI capex anxiety deepens:** Amazon's earnings flagged ballooning AI infrastructure spending, amplifying a theme that has now handed Nasdaq a ~$2 trillion October loss. Google/Alphabet also absorbed ~$500B in weekly market cap erosion. QQQ dropped 3.55% on the day.

**Fed hawk re-emerges:** St. Louis Fed's Musalem said another rate hike is needed "soon." The 10Y yield sits at 4.93% (down 1.2% on the day — a flight-to-quality bid), but the rate-hike signal reinforces pressure on growth equities.

**Oil surges, energy outperforms:** WTI crude +2.72% to $104.77, driven by Iran/Ukraine war-related refining demand. Chevron and Exxon hit all-time highs on surging refining profits — a rare bright spot in an ugly tape. Dow's relative resilience (-0.91%) largely reflects energy's weight.

**Gold hammered:** GC -3.15% to $3,904.50 — likely profit-taking and position liquidation amid broader risk-off deleveraging.

**Crypto rattled by $6.3B Binance hack:** Largest crypto theft on record initially spooked markets; BTC held relatively firm at $77,192 (-0.75%) and ETH -0.55% as losses were partially pared.

**VIX:** Jumped 20.87% to 20.79 — firmly out of complacency territory.
