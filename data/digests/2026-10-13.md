# Daily Market Brief — 2026-10-13

**TL;DR:** Markets drifted fractionally lower as chip-stock AI fatigue and a JPMorgan profit miss overshadowed solid Goldman/Citi beats, while the 10Y yield eased to 5.22% and crude held near $94.

## What Moved Markets

**Equities — narrow losses, tech dragging**
Indexes were nearly flat but small-caps and the Dow lagged more (-0.26–0.29%). The VIX ticked up 1.3% to 15.41, reflecting mild caution rather than fear. The AI/chip trade continued unwinding — semiconductors sliding enough to pull Nasdaq (-0.11%) below the broader tape.

**Bank earnings — mixed read**
- JPMorgan posted a rare profit miss as dealmaking and bond trading lost momentum — a notable data point given how consistently it has beaten.
- Goldman Sachs strong quarter; CNBC upgrading the stock.
- Citi beat estimates on investment banking, services, and wealth.

**Rates & dollar — modest relief**
10Y Treasury yield fell 4 bps to 5.22%, pausing its climb. Dollar Index slipped 0.39% to 102.18. PIMCO's Ivascyn warned yields have not yet peaked and the Fed may tighten further.

**Oil — geopolitical bid holds**
WTI +0.67% to $94.37. Paul Tudor Jones flagged a potential $20–25 further rally on Iran conflict risk. BP curtailed two Gulf of Mexico platforms ahead of Hurricane Isaias, adding a supply tightness angle.

**Bitcoin/Crypto — outperforming**
BTC +1.59% to $83,065; ETH +2.38% to $2,502. No single catalyst in headlines; functioning as a risk-on pocket while equities stalled.
