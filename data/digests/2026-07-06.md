## What Moved Markets

**Tech/Semis led:** Nasdaq outperformed sharply, lifted by a broad chip recovery. Broadcom surged ~6% on a new expanded Apple supply deal; AMD +10%, Intel +5% riding the same wave. NVDA added 0.83%. QQQ gained 1.70%.

**AI infrastructure pop:** TeraWulf spiked after signing a $19B data center lease with Anthropic — one of the largest AI infrastructure deals on record. Neocloud names broadly rallied in sympathy.

**Trump White House opening bell:** First-ever White House-hosted market open; Trump plugged Dell specifically, sending shares higher. He predicted stocks "going through the roof."

**Dow underperformed:** Microsoft's 4,800 job cuts (Xbox unit downsized, four gaming studios to be spun off) dragged MSFT -1.49%, weighing on the Dow while the rest of tech shrugged it off.

**Gold at $4,151, BTC retreating:** Gold +0.93% to a new high; Bitcoin -2.36% as Strategy sold $216M in BTC and central bank data showed 41 tonnes of gold bought in May vs. $8.9B in BTC ETF outflows.
