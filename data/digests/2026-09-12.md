# Daily Market Brief — 2026-09-12

**TL;DR:** Broad risk-on rally as VIX drops 11% and equities close ~1% higher, but oil slides back to $100 and the 10Y yield holds near 5%—tension between growth optimism and rate/geopolitical pressure remains the dominant theme.

---

## What moved markets

**Equities** posted a clean sweep: S&P +0.86% to 7,657, Nasdaq +0.96%, Dow +0.98%. Small caps lagged (Russell +0.45%), consistent with sensitivity to the near-5% 10Y yield. The VIX collapse from ~17.8 to 15.84 was the session's clearest signal—implied vol sold off hard, amplifying equity gains.

**Rates** are the counterweight. The 10Y yield ticked up another 3bps to 4.97%, just under the psychologically important 5% level. Headlines flagging an expected Fed rate hike and Dalio publicly urging investors to swap bonds for gold underscore how uncomfortable the fixed-income backdrop remains. Inflation outpacing wage growth again (per CNBC) adds pressure on the Fed's calculus.

**Oil** reversed sharply, -2.37% to $100.05—still round-number territory but fading from highs. Trump's public prediction that oil falls sharply after midterms (and after an Iran deal) appears to have weighed. WTI at exactly $100 is a level to watch.

**Dollar** was essentially flat (+0.03% to 99.12), offering no headwind to risk assets today.
