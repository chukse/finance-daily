# Daily Market Brief — 2026-08-11

**TL;DR:** Iran-Hormuz deadlock dominated, lifting gold +1.9% to $4,444 and pushing the 10-year yield up 8 bps to 4.70% while equities drifted fractionally lower ahead of July CPI.

---

## What Moved Markets

**Iran/Hormuz:** The dominant macro story. US and Iran hardened positions, deal prospects faded, and WTI climbed to ~$82.35 (+0.27%) intraday — headlines noted prices touching $84 at points before easing. Gold surged +1.88% to $4,444, reflecting geopolitical risk bid. Treasury yields rose alongside oil's inflation signal, with the 10Y at 4.699% (+8 bps), weighing on rate-sensitive equities.

**Equities:** Losses were narrow but skewed toward growth and small caps. Russell 2000 led declines at -0.56%, consistent with the higher-yield, risk-off tilt. VIX barely moved (-0.13% to 15.44), signaling no panic.

**CPI watch:** JPMorgan published a note on how markets may react to July's CPI print; Fed's Schmid publicly flagged inflation as his "primary concern" and supported keeping rates higher. Mortgage rates sitting at 6.69%.
