# Daily Market Brief — 2026-10-28

**TL;DR:** Markets drifted near flat as oil surged 3% to $103, the 10Y yield cracked 5%, and traders held their breath ahead of Fed, Microsoft, and Alphabet results.

---

## What Moved Markets

**Oil & rates dominated the macro tape.** WTI crude jumped $3.02 (+3%) to $102.92 — EIA data showed falling crude and distillate inventories, while European gas topped €100 on Middle East escalation fears. The 10Y Treasury yield pushed to 5.02% (+5.4 bps), a psychologically significant level that capped equity upside. The dollar slipped fractionally (-0.07%) despite the yield move, suggesting some safe-haven flows going elsewhere.

**Equities were essentially unchanged.** S&P +0.20%, Dow +0.27%, Nasdaq near flat (+0.01%), Russell 2000 +0.08%. VIX eased to 17.38, suggesting the market is nervous but not panicking. Breadth was thin; the session was clearly a placeholder ahead of the Fed decision and Big Tech earnings.

**Gold and crypto both dipped modestly.** Gold fell -0.49% to $4,054 — unusual given the oil/geopolitical backdrop, likely pressured by the 5% 10Y yield. BTC slipped -0.50% to $80,402.
