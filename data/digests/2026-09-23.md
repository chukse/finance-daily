# Daily Market Brief — 2026-09-23

**TL;DR:** A 19-year high on the 10-year Treasury yield (5.07%) dragged equities lower across the board, with the Dow leading declines at -0.85%.

---

## What moved markets

**Rates:** The 10-year Treasury yield jumped 11 bps to 5.071%, its highest since 2007, after hot economic data reinforced a "higher for longer" rate narrative. That's the primary driver of today's broad selloff and strength in the dollar (+0.61% to 101.04).

**Equities:** All major indices fell in a tight band (-0.48% to -0.85%), with the Dow underperforming. VIX actually fell 1.55% to 14.64, suggesting the move was orderly rather than panicked.

**Oil:** WTI crude dropped 3.10% to $91.66. Iran floated conditions for reopening the Strait of Hormuz, easing supply-disruption fears. A potential diesel export ban and the Goldman Sachs rate/oil nexus are in the background.

**Gold & Bitcoin:** Both sold off — gold -1.11% to $4,328, BTC -2.02% to $84,435 — consistent with a stronger dollar and rising real yields pulling capital toward Treasuries.
