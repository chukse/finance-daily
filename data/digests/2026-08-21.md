# Daily Market Brief — 2026-08-21

**TL;DR:** Rising Treasury yields (10Y at 4.696%, +9bp) hammered equities for a weekly loss while gold surged 3.1% to $4,654 and Bitcoin jumped 5.4% toward a 20% weekly gain.

## What Moved Markets

**Rates pressure:** The 10Y yield's climb to 4.70% was the primary catalyst for broad equity weakness. Debt-ceiling/fiscal concerns and a MarketWatch piece flagging a "wrenching" U.S. debt crackdown reinforced bond jitters. The Treasury's buyback plans were brushed off by markets.

**Equities:** All four major indices fell 0.9–1.3%, with small-caps (Russell 2000 -1.34%) and the Dow (-1.32%) leading losses. The S&P is on track for its biggest weekly decline since mid-July. VIX fell 3.4% to 15.5 despite the selloff—suggesting orderly de-risking rather than panic. Futures are pointing higher Friday evening.

**Gold:** +3.1% to $4,654, on track for a 4%+ weekly gain. Drivers: weaker dollar (DXY -0.24% to 98.66), fiscal anxiety, and technical momentum to three-month highs.

**Bitcoin:** +5.45% to $77,012; ETH +2.1%. Bitcoin is tracking ~20% weekly gain on renewed investor optimism. Crypto decoupling sharply from equities.

**Oil:** WTI -1.6% to $86.46, slipping on the day but still holding significant weekly gains on Venezuela supply headlines and geopolitical tension.

**Macro data:** Eurozone PMIs showed business activity growing at the fastest pace since November. UK services sector also beat. Both support a "rest of world outperforming" narrative vs. U.S. fiscal drag.
