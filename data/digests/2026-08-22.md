# Daily Market Brief — 2026-08-22

**TL;DR:** S&P 500 hit a record close at 7,674 as the Dow led gains and VIX dropped 5.5%, but gold's surge to $4,624 (+2.4%) and a 10Y yield touching 4.74% signal deepening unease beneath the surface calm.

---

## What Moved Markets

**Broad rally, divergent signals.** All four major indices advanced — Dow +0.98%, Russell 2000 +0.85%, S&P and Nasdaq both +0.43%. VIX fell to 15.1, consistent with a risk-on session. But the bond market told a different story: the 10Y yield rose to 4.74% (+9 bps), near decade highs, with headlines noting Treasuries' safe-haven status is visibly eroding.

**Gold surging, dollar steady.** Gold at $4,624 is up 2.4% on the day — a striking move driven by the Iran-U.S. tension flare (Trump threatening to bomb Oman, new sanctions imminent, Iran truce expiration) and fading confidence in dollar-denominated safe havens. The DXY slipped only marginally to 98.80.

**Geopolitics: Iran front and center.** Iran granted Iraqi oil tanker passage through Hormuz, preventing an immediate supply shock, which likely capped crude's downside; WTI fell 0.88% to $87.06. But the situation remains live — hard-liners reportedly preparing for prolonged conflict even as top politicians signal openness to talks.

**U.S.-Canada trade war deepens.** No tariff deal reached; both sides dug in. A negative catalyst for risk appetite at the margin, though markets shrugged on the day.

**Chip stocks weighed on tech.** WSJ flagged "Chip Stocks Clobbered" as a theme this week. Boeing engineers also rejected a four-year contract, adding labor cost risk.

**Crypto sold off.** Bitcoin -1.57% to $77,103; Ethereum -3.71% to $2,422. Rising real yields continuing to pressure speculative assets.
