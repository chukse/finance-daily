# Daily Market Brief — 2026-10-21

**Daily Brief — Wednesday, October 21, 2026**

**TL;DR:** SpaceX earnings beat and Tesla/SpaceX merger speculation drove a Nasdaq-led rally; oil cratered 4.8% on Gulf supply recovery and Ukraine-Russia supply dynamics.

---

## What Moved Markets

**Equities** — Risk-on session, tech leading. Nasdaq +1.09%, S&P 500 +0.52%, Dow lagged at +0.29%. VIX dropped 3.5% to 16.7, confirming broad calm. Russell 2000 +0.47% tracked the broader advance.

**SpaceX/Tesla catalyst** — SpaceX shares jumped ~8% after an earnings beat and Elon Musk's public hints at a Tesla tie-up. That single narrative lifted sentiment across AI/tech hardware. Super Micro added fuel with an upbeat forecast citing robust AI demand.

**Rates** — 10Y Treasury yield slipped to 5.11% (from ~5.12%), a modest relief. Still historically elevated and a headwind for rate-sensitives; mortgage demand fell even as rates dipped for the first time in two months.

**Oil** — WTI -4.79% to $96.91, largest single-day drop in recent sessions. Traders reversed early gains on signs of Gulf supply recovery. Ukraine drone strikes on Russian infrastructure had briefly supported prices but the net read was bearish supply-side.

**Dollar/Gold/Crypto** — DXY roughly flat at 101.81. Gold +0.21% to $4,151. Bitcoin +1.83% to $84,462; Ethereum +2.50% to $2,508, riding the risk-on wave.

**Europe** — Eurozone bond yields rising; French borrowing costs hit a 24-year high, adding peripheral stress to EU fixed income.
