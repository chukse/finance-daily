# Daily Market Brief — 2026-08-03

**TL;DR:** Trump's cancellation of planned Iran strikes collapsed oil 7.3% and lifted equities broadly, while Microsoft and Alphabet surged on earnings beats.

---

## What Moved Markets

**Geopolitics drove everything.** Trump called off planned military strikes on Iran and signaled resumption of nuclear talks, triggering a sharp risk repricing: the S&P 500 +0.91%, Dow +1.21% (+~640 pts), Nasdaq +1.23%. The direct read-through hit crude hardest — WTI fell $6.19 to $78.49, a 7.3% single-day drop, as Hormuz closure fears receded. The 10Y Treasury yield fell 5.5 bps to 4.69% on the same de-escalation logic.

**Gold held gains (+0.93% to $4,087)** despite the risk-on tone, suggesting residual skepticism about deal durability. VIX edged up 1.81% to 16.3 — unusual divergence from equities, likely reflecting event-risk hedging ahead of Iran talks Monday.

**Dollar flat** (-0.03% to 99.77) but the session featured unusual FX action: Reuters reported the U.S. intervened to support the yen by selling euros, a structurally unconventional move that rattled currency desks.

**JPMorgan warned** that Fed Chair Warsh's failure to reinforce Fed credibility may force a rate *hike* before year-end — a tail risk the bond market isn't pricing.
