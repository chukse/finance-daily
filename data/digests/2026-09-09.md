# Daily Market Brief — 2026-09-09

**TL;DR:** Oil surging past $100/bbl on US-Iran hostilities hammered stocks across the board, pushed yields to 4.82%, and sent gold to fresh highs above $4,400.

---

## What Moved Markets

**Oil shock dominates.** WTI settled near $96.37 (+3.59%) on the day, but intraday prints crossed $101 as fresh US-Iran military exchanges struck Saudi energy infrastructure. Brent crossed $100. Reuters flags the Strait of Hormuz as the key chokepoint—roughly 20% of global seaborne oil transits it. Gasoline prices hit a Labor Day record.

**Rates followed oil higher.** The 10Y Treasury yield climbed 2 bps to 4.82%. The Treasury Department's unusual move—buying back $6B in longer-term debt, triple normal volume—signals active market management at elevated rate levels. Trump publicly pressured the Fed for cuts; experts pushed back, noting inflation risks.

**Equities sold off broadly.** The Dow shed ~350 points (-0.78%). Small-caps led declines (Russell 2000 -0.84%), consistent with rate-sensitive and consumer-exposed names feeling the squeeze. VIX ticked up to 16.1 (+2.16%)—elevated but not panicked.

**Gold and dollar diverge.** Gold +1.03% to $4,439, classic geopolitical/inflation hedge bid. The dollar was essentially flat (-0.06%), suggesting the move is commodity/risk driven rather than a pure flight to USD.

## Stories Worth Knowing

**Apple's foldable iPhone debuts.** The company held its fall event with CEO Ternus leading—first foldable iPhone and new Watch series were the headliners. Stocks shrugged.

**AMD's CFO raises AI chip market forecast to $3 trillion.** AMD shares rose ~3% on the update; Intel edged higher sympathetically. Susquehanna separately notes memory now exceeds 50% of total semiconductor revenue.

**CoreWeave demand > supply.** CEO confirmed the company can't keep up with Nvidia chip demand, reinforcing the AI infrastructure buildout narrative even as data center tax breaks face state-level clawbacks.

**Google commits $15B to AI infrastructure, buys Finnish nuclear power.** Underscores the scale of hyperscaler energy requirements as AI workloads expand.

**Robinhood becomes an IPO underwriter.** The firm takes an underwriting role in the Oura (smart ring) IPO—a meaningful step beyond its retail brokerage identity.
