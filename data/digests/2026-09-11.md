# Daily Market Brief — 2026-09-11

**TL;DR:** Stocks rallied sharply as oil's retreat from $100 and a slightly-better-than-feared CPI print let traders look past sticky inflation, with the VIX dropping 10% and 10-year yields pulling back from the 5% threshold.

---

## What Moved Markets

**Equities** S&P 500 +0.93% to 7,662, Nasdaq +1.12%, Dow +467 pts. Broad-based bid with tech leading. Risk-on tone reinforced by VIX collapsing to 16.02 (-10.2%).

**Inflation & Rates** August CPI came in "firm" but apparently not as bad as the most hawkish scenarios — yields on the 10-year pulled back 2-3 bps to 4.93%, retreating from the psychologically significant 5% level that had rattled markets earlier in the week. Despite the relief, Fed rate hike odds for the Sept. 16 FOMC meeting surged to 85%+, meaning a hike is nearly fully priced.

**Oil** WTI fell 2.80% to $99.61 after back-to-back weeks of double-digit gains that briefly pushed Brent above $100. Houthis reportedly advancing toward a key Red Sea island, keeping geopolitical risk elevated — the pullback reflects profit-taking, not a resolved supply threat. $6 diesel is now a real consumer/freight story.

**Gold & Bitcoin** Gold +1.10% to $4,413 — stagflation hedge bid. Bitcoin +2.72% to $78,651; Ethereum outperformed sharply at +7.00% to $2,608.

**Dollar** DX essentially flat at 99.00 (-0.09%) — notable that the dollar didn't rally on hot CPI, suggesting the move is already well-priced.
