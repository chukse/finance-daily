# Daily Market Brief — 2026-07-02

**TL;DR:** Weak June jobs report (+57K vs. ~180K expected) pushed yields higher paradoxically as the dollar fell, gold surged to $4,131, and tech split — MSFT led on a $2.5B AI venture while NVDA slid.

---

## What Moved Markets

**Jobs miss dominates the tape.** The U.S. added only 57,000 jobs in June, a sharp undershoot, with unemployment slipping to 4.2%. The read was stagflation-tinged: the 10Y yield *rose* 2.26% to 4.47% (possibly on supply/fiscal concerns or a short squeeze), while the dollar dropped 0.60% to 100.78 — an unusual divergence suggesting the market is pricing risk rather than a clean "bad data = lower rates" trade.

**Equities soft but orderly.** S&P 500 -0.22%, Nasdaq -0.66%, Dow nearly flat (-0.03%). VIX fell 2.47% to 16.18 — no panic. The pre-holiday July 4th session likely kept volume thin and moves muted.

**Gold and crypto caught the safe-haven/risk bid simultaneously.** Gold +1.55% to $4,131, a fresh record vicinity. BTC +2.86% to $61,718; ETH +4.11% to $1,675 — both responding to dollar weakness and the soft macro print.

**Oil continued lower.** WTI -1.52% to $67.54, third straight down day, driven by progress in U.S.-Iran Doha talks reducing supply-disruption premium.
