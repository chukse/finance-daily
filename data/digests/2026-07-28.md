# Daily Market Brief — 2026-07-28

**TL;DR:** Chip stocks dragged Nasdaq lower on AI-spending skepticism ahead of mega-cap earnings, while strong results from UPS, Coca-Cola, and Boeing's revenue beat kept the Dow and small-caps bid.

---

## What Moved Markets

**Chip sell-off / AI anxiety** dominated the session. NVDA fell 4.99%, with Micron and SK Hynix cited alongside a Supermicro chip-smuggling scandal drawing in Nvidia. Broader concern that AI capex returns remain unproven is weighing on the sector ahead of Meta, Microsoft, and Amazon reporting this week — Google's recent results apparently set a skeptical tone.

**Earnings split the tape.** Coca-Cola beat and raised full-year guidance (Diet Coke cited specifically). UPS beat and raised. Boeing missed on the bottom line — Air Force One cost overruns weighed — but revenue topped estimates, backlog is healthy, and cash turned positive; stock rose anyway. PayPal beat estimates and the CEO left the door open on M&A.

**Rates and commodities eased.** 10Y yield slipped 1.2 bps to 4.629%, providing modest relief. WTI crude fell 1.66% to $81.24 on Iran-Gulf diplomatic progress (Oman presented a Hormuz fee proposal; Trump flagged "good talks") and Trump's claim of $13B in Venezuela oil sales adding supply context. Gold dropped 1.04% to $4,032 — still well below $4,100 — ahead of today's Fed meeting. Dollar edged up 0.05% to 101.52, near a one-month high.

**Fed meeting** is today's macro centerpiece; markets appear to be pricing in no hike but watching language closely.
