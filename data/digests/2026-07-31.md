# Daily Market Brief — 2026-07-31

**TL;DR:** Microsoft's blowout quarter and Amazon's cloud surge drove a broad tech rally, with an Iran war oil premium and Fed dissenter hawkishness adding macro texture.

---

## What Moved Markets

**Tech earnings dominated.** The Nasdaq led all indices, up +2.78%, with QQQ gaining +3.30%. Microsoft surged +15.51% — the single biggest individual driver — on strong earnings. Amazon added +3.90% as AWS cloud revenue allayed concerns about AI spending ROI. The S&P 500 closed at 7,437, up +1.66%; the Dow gained +1.19%.

**Apple was the drag.** AAPL fell -1.41% after earnings flagged supply chain disruptions and a softer forecast; Tim Cook's final earnings call as CEO drew mixed analyst reactions. Google dipped -0.91%.

**Oil surged on Iran war premium.** WTI crude +2.28% to $85.50. Exxon and Chevron profits "quadrupled" in Q2 per headlines, with the Hormuz Strait situation still constraining supply despite partial traffic recovery. Energy was a clear sector winner.

**Fed hawks added yield pressure.** Dissenting Fed officials publicly defended their rate hike votes, citing inflation urgency. The 10Y yield ticked up to 4.70% (+0.79%). VIX edged up to 17.3 despite the equity rally — a mild divergence worth watching.

**Dollar firmed; gold softened.** DXY +0.33% to 100.34. Gold slipped -0.16% to $4,094 after briefly topping $4,100 overnight when the U.S. paused airstrikes.

**Crypto sold off.** BTC -1.55% to $63,722; ETH -1.66% to $1,886 — risk-off within crypto even as equities rallied.

## Stories Worth Knowing

- **Iran war escalation:** Iran struck U.S. assets in Kuwait and Bahrain after an Egypt drone attack; Trump convened Cabinet at Camp David. Direct impact: oil premium, gold safe-haven bid, and continued Hormuz shipping uncertainty.

- **Tesla/SpaceX merger speculation:** WSJ reports Tesla is weighing a sale of its China business as a precondition for a potential SpaceX combination — a story with significant structural implications for both companies.

- **Fed dissent goes public:** Multiple officials who voted to hike rates are now explaining themselves in the press. This is unusual transparency and signals the hiking debate inside the FOMC is live, not resolved.

- **South Korean chipmakers' biggest-ever rally:** Samsung and SK Hynix surged on AI memory demand — reinforces the NVDA/AI infrastructure trade has global legs, and rising memory costs are squeezing downstream AI builders.

- **Universal Music -25%:** Shares collapsed on streaming concern headlines — a sharp single-day move in a major media name that didn't make the main indices move but signals real pressure on music licensing economics.
