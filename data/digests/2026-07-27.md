# Daily Market Brief — 2026-07-27

**TL;DR:** U.S.-Iran ceasefire pause crushed oil 6% and lit up rate-sensitive equities, with the Dow surging 600 points while tech diverged sharply on mega-cap rotation.

---

## What moved markets

**The driver:** A pause in U.S.-Iran hostilities sent WTI crude down 6.0% to $83.92, its largest single-day drop in two months. Lower energy prices compressed inflation expectations, pulling the 10Y Treasury yield down 5.6 bps to 4.647% and sending the VIX down 4.8% to 17.8.

**Equity rotation:** The rate relief trade played out clearly in the spread — Dow +1.46%, Russell 2000 +1.05%, S&P 500 +0.68%, while the Nasdaq eked out only +0.04% and QQQ actually fell 0.52%. Value and small-caps outperformed growth. The S&P is tracking record Q2 profits per earnings season data.

**Gold/Dollar/Crypto:** Gold added 0.55% to $4,090 despite the risk-on tone. Dollar flat at 101.41. Bitcoin unchanged at $65,342; ETH +0.56%. No meaningful crypto catalyst.
