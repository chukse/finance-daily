# Daily Brief — August 1, 2026

**TL;DR:** Amazon surges 15% on blowout earnings while Microsoft sets a near-$450B single-day market-cap record, powering mega-cap tech higher even as Apple crashes 7% and small-caps fade.

---

## What Moved Markets

**Mega-cap tech divergence dominated.** Amazon (+15.3%) and Alphabet (+6.7%) posted massive earnings-driven gains; Microsoft (+3.0%) and Nvidia (+2.9%) rode the AI tailwind. Apple (-7.4%) was the glaring outlier — likely a guidance or margin disappointment — dragging enough index weight to cap S&P gains at +0.70% despite the tech surge.

**Large vs. small split.** Russell 2000 fell 0.5% while the S&P and Nasdaq gained, a classic risk-rotation pattern where capital concentrates in high-conviction growth names rather than broad cyclicals.

**Rates and the dollar.** The 10Y yield climbed 8 bps to 4.745%, adding pressure on rate-sensitive small-caps and gold (-1.2%). The dollar slipped slightly (-0.21%), partly on reported coordinated US-Japan yen intervention.

**Oil firm on Middle East escalation.** WTI +1.3% to $84.67 as Iranian forces fired on tankers near Oman and threatened to choke shipping lanes. Indian Oil Corp rushed to boost spot purchases. OPEC+ output hike talk is not cooling prices.

**VIX down 6.4% to 16.0** — the market is pricing out near-term fear despite the geopolitical noise, likely on the strength of earnings beats.
