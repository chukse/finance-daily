# Daily Market Brief — 2026-08-17

**TL;DR:** Large-caps slipped on geopolitical risk and rising yields while gold surged 1.76% to $4,458 and small-caps bucked the trend.

## What moved markets

**Rates & dollar:** 10Y Treasury yield climbed 1.19% to 4.697%, sustaining pressure on growth equities. The dollar softened 0.18% to 99.49, its lowest since early June as Fed rate-hike bets fade — Goldman Sachs explicitly called no September hike.

**Geopolitics:** Trump threatened to bomb Oman as the U.S.-Iran ceasefire expired, lifting safe havens. Gold jumped $77 to $4,458, a standout move. WTI held near flat at $82.55 despite Iran escalation headlines — market isn't pricing a major supply disruption yet.

**Equities split:** S&P 500 -0.17%, Nasdaq -0.28%, Dow -0.20% — all marginal. Russell 2000 +0.51% outperformed, consistent with the weakening dollar and no-hike expectations benefiting rate-sensitive small-caps. VIX ticked up 4.63% to 14.91, a modest but notable caution signal.

**Crypto:** Bitcoin +1.23% to $63,594; Ethereum +1.55% to $1,903. Trump-linked crypto venture received a preliminary bank charter nod, adding a sector-specific tailwind.
