# Daily Market Brief — 2026-07-14

**TL;DR:** IBM crashed 23%+ on an earnings miss while tech dragged Nasdaq down 1.55%, but the real macro story was softer-than-expected June CPI (3.5%) colliding with a 3.2% oil spike on Trump's Strait of Hormuz threat.

---

## What Moved Markets

**Inflation & rates:** June CPI came in at 3.5% YoY — below consensus — driven by easing energy prices. The 10Y yield fell 6 bps to 4.55%. Fed Chair Warsh used congressional testimony to pledge zero tolerance for persistent inflation and cited AI investment as a disinflationary tailwind. Despite the softer print, the oil shock muddied the Fed's path.

**Oil & geopolitics:** WTI surged 3.16% to $80.61 after Trump claimed the U.S. is "taking over" the Strait of Hormuz. Iran tensions simultaneously sent gold up 2.44% to $4,094 and the dollar down 0.53% to 100.74. Silver fell, breaking from gold.

**IBM collapse:** IBM plunged ~23% — its worst day in nearly 40 years — after a surprise Q2 earnings miss. That single name weighed on the Dow, which still held up best at -0.26% vs. Nasdaq's -1.55%.

**Bank earnings:** JPMorgan posted record profit on dealmaking and trading gains. Bank of America beat on a 27% profit jump, citing a "healthy economic backdrop." Both stocks were mixed despite strong results — market already priced the good news.

**Equities broad:** S&P -0.79%, Nasdaq -1.55%, Russell -0.83%. VIX fell 3.67% to 16.5 despite equity weakness, suggesting the selloff was orderly. Eye-popping earnings expectations have failed to lift the market for two months (per Santoli).

**Crypto:** BTC +2.3% to $63,668; ETH +3.83% to $1,841. Coinbase upgraded to Buy by US Tiger Securities.

## Stories Worth Knowing

- **IBM -23%** is the stock's worst single-day drop since the late 1980s. Earnings miss was a surprise; timing matters as tech valuations are stretched.
- **Hormuz leverage:** Trump's Strait of Hormuz claim is a significant escalation variable for energy markets. If credible, it re-introduces a persistent oil risk premium just as inflation was cooling.
- **CPI vs. oil tension:** Softer CPI would normally cement a rate-cut path, but a sustained oil rebound at $80+ could push headline inflation back up by Q3. Watch next month's energy component closely.
- **Warsh tone:** New Fed chair is signaling hawkish credibility-building — "no tolerance" language is deliberate messaging, not just boilerplate.
- **Fintech bid:** Analysts raised targets on PayPal, Robinhood, and upgraded Coinbase today — a cluster of positive fintech sentiment worth tracking ahead of earnings season.
