# Daily Market Brief — 2026-08-14

**TL;DR:** S&P 500 closed above 7,800 for the first time as softening inflation data pulled the 10Y yield down 8 bps, lifting growth stocks and sending gold to $4,428.

---

## What Moved Markets

**Risk-on, rates down:** The 10Y Treasury yield fell 8.8 bps to 4.641%, the day's clearest catalyst. Softer-than-expected inflation data (per gold's framing as an inflation hedge) drove the move, compressing the dollar (-0.34% to 99.62) and boosting equities broadly.

**Equities:** S&P 500 +0.65% to 7,799, Nasdaq +0.81%. Tech led; small caps lagged (+0.24%). VIX dipped to 14.51, consistent with complacency near record highs.

**Gold:** +1.47% to $4,428 — the standout commodity move, explicitly linked to softening inflation readings. WTI crude +0.50% to $81.66; oil on track for a significant weekly gain amid stalled Strait of Hormuz talks, though a deal is reportedly closing in.

**Hormuz / Iran:** Two more ships attacked near Hormuz; U.S. threatened stepped-up economic sanctions. Negotiators nonetheless said to be nearing a deal to reopen the strait — the tension/resolution tug-of-war is the week's dominant geopolitical trade.

**Bitcoin:** -1.09% to $62,712, diverging from the risk-on equity tape. ETH -0.55%.
