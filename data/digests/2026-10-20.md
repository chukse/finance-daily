# Daily Market Brief — 2026-10-20

**TL;DR:** A 5.9% crude oil plunge on Middle East diplomacy hopes lifted the Dow 500+ points and dragged inflation fears lower, powering a broad risk-on session led by small caps.

---

## What moved markets

**Crude oil** was the day's dominant macro variable. WTI fell $6.07 to $97.60 (-5.85%) on reports of diplomatic progress in the Middle East, unwinding weeks of geopolitical risk premium. Lower energy prices eased inflation concerns, pulling the **10Y Treasury yield** down 3 bps to 5.106% and compressing the VIX to 18.0 (-2.4%).

**Equities rotated broadly higher** but with a clear value/cyclical tilt. The **Russell 2000 led at +1.70%**, consistent with a rate-relief trade benefiting rate-sensitive small caps. The **Dow outperformed at +0.93%** vs. Nasdaq's modest +0.20%, signaling rotation away from high-multiple tech.

**Earnings** provided selective support. **General Motors** beat Q3 estimates and narrowed full-year guidance, though headlines flagged Iran conflict cost pressures as a headwind—stock reaction was mixed per conflicting reports. **GE Aerospace** cited sustained high demand despite supply chain friction. **Northrop Grumman** guided to accelerating growth on defense demand.

**Data center/AI names** remained a drag after a Korea-listed data center IPO plunged 30%, reinforcing concerns about AI infrastructure valuations. The **dollar** slipped 0.23% to 101.74, a mild tailwind for multinationals.

**Housing** continued to deteriorate: pending home sales hit a **25-year low**, housing starts fell again in September, and new home builder traffic dropped to a **14-year low** with price cuts accelerating.
