# Daily Market Brief — 2026-08-06

**Daily Brief — August 6, 2026**

**TL;DR:** Tech sold off hard on earnings disappointments and a Strait of Hormuz reopening deal in progress lifted the Dow, while gold hit $4,312 on safe-haven demand and Alphabet plunged 4% ahead of a $25B bond sale.

---

## What Moved Markets

**Rotation out of tech.** Nasdaq fell 0.83%, Russell 2000 -0.59%, while the Dow gained 0.49% — a classic defensive rotation. Hedge funds reportedly forced out of tech positions (per MarketWatch) may be amplifying the move.

**Earnings drag on software/storage.** Sandisk and Western Digital led losses after disappointing results; Datadog also a notable loser. Salesforce declined as markets questioned SaaS durability. Fiserv dropped after a "bruising reset."

**Strait of Hormuz.** Traders are monitoring negotiations to reopen the strait; Dow futures rose on the headline. WTI crude barely moved (-0.03% to $75.20), suggesting the market is waiting for confirmation.

**Rates and gold.** 10Y Treasury yield rose 4bp to 4.647%, pressuring growth stocks. Gold surged 1.56% to $4,312 — a record-vicinity level — combining rate-anxiety and geopolitical bids.

**Alphabet's $25B bond issuance** hit sentiment on top of the -4% price drop; large bond sales signal balance sheet deployment but dilute equity perception near-term.
