# Daily Market Brief — 2026-08-28

**Daily Brief — Friday, August 28, 2026**

**TL;DR:** Fed Chair Warsh's Jackson Hole warning that the Fed "may have work to do" on inflation rattled rates-sensitive assets, pushing the 10Y yield up 4.7 bps to 4.72% and hammering small-caps, gold, and crypto while mega-cap tech mostly shrugged it off.

---

## What Moved Markets

**The Warsh shock.** The dominant story. Speaking at Jackson Hole, Fed Chair Kevin Warsh struck a hawkish tone — flagging persistent inflation concerns and signaling rate cuts are not imminent. The 2-year yield jumped sharply; the 10Y settled at 4.72% (+1.03%). The dollar index gained 0.52% to 99.68.

**Small-caps bore the brunt.** Russell 2000 fell 1.39% — far outpacing S&P 500 (-0.25%) and Dow (-0.02%) declines — as higher-for-longer rates disproportionately pressure rate-sensitive smaller companies with floating-rate debt.

**Gold and crypto sold off hard.** Gold dropped 2.29% to $4,504, its biggest one-day move in weeks, as real yields rose and the dollar strengthened. Bitcoin fell 3.57% to $77,396; Ethereum shed 3.26% to $2,429 — both repricing hawkish Fed expectations.

**VIX hit a YTD low** intraday (settled at 14.43, -0.55%), suggesting equity traders view Warsh's comments as credible and stabilizing rather than panic-inducing. The bond market's calm reaction reinforced this: yields rose but spreads held.

**Ag commodities surged.** Corn and wheat jumped to their highest levels in more than three years — supply concerns, not directly Fed-related.

**Week was still positive** for the S&P 500 despite Friday's dip.
