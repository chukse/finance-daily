# Daily Market Brief — 2026-07-17

**TL;DR:** Tech selloff deepens on chip weakness, Netflix earnings miss, and escalating Iran strikes — VIX jumps 10% as investors rotate defensively.

---

## What Moved Markets

**Semiconductors & Big Tech led the decline.** Nasdaq fell 1.47%, underperforming the S&P 500 (-0.51%) and Dow (-0.20%). NVDA dropped 2.40%, AMZN -1.99%, GOOGL -4.44%. The chip selloff was broad and deepening into the session.

**Netflix dragged sentiment further.** Shares tumbled ~9-11% premarket on a weak earnings forecast, adding to the risk-off tone across growth stocks.

**Iran escalation pushed oil up sharply.** WTI crude +1.85% to $80.41, with headlines showing Iran strikes hitting Kuwait's water and power infrastructure and expanding attacks to Syria and Bahrain. Oil is up ~13% on the week; $100/barrel is being discussed. Gold was essentially flat at $3,985 despite the geopolitical noise — notable given it was cited falling to Nov. '25 lows in earlier headlines, suggesting some intraday recovery.

**Treasuries rallied on safe-haven demand.** 10Y yield fell 0.96% to 4.525%, consistent with the flight from risk assets.

**VIX spiked 10%** to 18.41 — not extreme but a meaningful one-day jump signaling rising near-term uncertainty.

**Dollar barely moved** (+0.10% to 100.83), suggesting the safe-haven bid went to Treasuries rather than USD.

**SpaceX IPO stumbled further** after a Starship test flight abort, with shares sliding below IPO price.
