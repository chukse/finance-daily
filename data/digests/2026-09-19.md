# Daily Market Brief — 2026-09-19

**Daily Brief — September 19, 2026**

**TL;DR:** The Fed hiked rates again, pushing the 10Y yield to 5.0% and rotating money into large-cap growth while small caps and bonds sold off.

---

## What Moved Markets

**Fed rate hike dominates.** The Federal Reserve under Chair Warsh raised rates, confirming its hawkish credibility. The 10Y Treasury yield jumped +1.03% to **5.00%** — a psychologically significant level. WSJ and MarketWatch note Wall Street is now treating the hike as a signal the Fed is serious about inflation, not a panic trigger.

**Divergent equity reaction.** Large-cap growth (Nasdaq +0.39%, S&P +0.17%) held up; rate-sensitive small caps (Russell 2000 -0.50%) and the Dow (-0.18%) lagged. The VIX fell -4.08% to 14.81, suggesting the hike was largely priced in.

**Oil slips below $100.** WTI crude fell -1.58% to **$100.30**, despite Middle East tension (flames reported near Riyadh's airport; Pakistan pressing Iran). Headline risk remains but supply fears aren't spiking.

**Gold hits $4,425.** Up +0.57%, gold continues its 2026 bull run — likely absorbing both rate-hike uncertainty and geopolitical risk premium.

**Dollar flat.** DXY at 100.22 barely moved (+0.00%), unusual given the rate hike — suggests markets had fully discounted it or offsetting global flows are neutralizing USD demand.
