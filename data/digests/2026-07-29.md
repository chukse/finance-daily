# Daily Market Brief — 2026-07-29

**TL;DR:** A fresh round of U.S.-Saudi airstrikes on Iran-backed targets sent WTI crude surging 6.8% and tilted the session defensive, lifting the Dow while dragging the Nasdaq lower ahead of tonight's Fed decision.

---

## What Moved Markets

**Oil & geopolitics** dominated. WTI jumped $5.42 to $84.67 after Trump said the U.S. would "hit Iran hard" and airstrikes resumed against Iran-backed militias in Iraq, snapping a fragile diplomatic stretch. Energy was the clear sector winner; the move pushed the 10Y yield up 2.5 bps to 4.629% (supply/inflation anxiety) and gold added 0.98% to $4,076.

**Rotation, not rally.** The Dow (+1.03%) outperformed sharply versus the Nasdaq (-0.22%) and QQQ (-0.97%), a classic risk-off rotation into value/industrials from growth. The VIX rose 2.86% to 18.73, confirming elevated nerves rather than panic.

**Fed on hold but not quiet.** The FOMC decision is live today; consensus expects no change, but headlines note dissension within the Warsh-led Fed. A surprise hike is being discussed at the margin. Markets are pricing caution—dollar barely moved (+0.09% to 101.47).

**Chip stocks weighed on tech.** SK Hynix posted record profits but shares slid; the sector broadly gave back ground, with the broader "chip stocks shed $1T" narrative from earlier in the week still lingering. NVDA managed only +0.25%.

**P&G miss.** Procter & Gamble reported revenue below estimates with flat volumes and issued a soft fiscal-year outlook—a consumer spending warning sign. At least one institutional holder exited the position.
