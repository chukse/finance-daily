# Daily Market Brief — 2026-09-04

**TL;DR:** A blowout August jobs report (+162K vs. ~120K expected, unemployment steady at 4.1%) reignited Fed rate-hike fears, pushing the 2-year yield to its highest since January 2025 and dragging most assets lower.

---

## What Moved Markets

**Jobs report dominated everything.** August nonfarm payrolls came in at 162,000 — well above consensus — flipping market pricing toward the possibility of another Fed hike rather than cuts. The 10-year yield rose 4 bps to 4.78%; the 2-year moved more aggressively (highest since Jan 2025). Rate-sensitive assets sold off across the board.

**Equities.** Broad indices fell but not dramatically — S&P 500 -0.49%, Dow -0.69% (≈300 points), Nasdaq -0.46%. The Russell 2000 was nearly flat (-0.01%), an unusual divergence; small caps may be pricing in less Fed sensitivity at current levels. VIX actually dipped to 14.2, suggesting this is repositioning rather than panic.

**Rates & dollar.** 10Y at 4.78% is the key pressure point. Dollar Index edged up 0.14% to 99.13 — muted given the data surprise, possibly capped by Trump's public threat to cut trade with countries running surpluses with the U.S. unless the Fed slashes rates.

**Crypto sold off.** Bitcoin -2.83% to $78,983, Ethereum -2.61% — consistent with risk-off and higher real-rate pressure. Both had been on a three-week winning streak per headlines.

**Commodities.** WTI crude -0.56% to $90.79 despite Hormuz tanker attacks (two vessels hit overnight), suggesting the geopolitical risk premium is not yet pricing in escalation. Gold -0.54% to $4,467, pressured by the stronger dollar and higher real yields.

## Stories Worth Knowing

- **Trump vs. Fed:** Trump publicly threatened to halt trade with countries running surpluses with the U.S. if the Fed doesn't cut rates — an unusual direct pressure campaign that complicates the Fed's policy signaling environment.
- **Tesla Cybercab probe:** NHTSA opened a formal investigation into nearly 1,000 Cybercabs for federal safety compliance. Early-stage but adds headline risk to Tesla's autonomous rollout timeline.
- **Adobe CEO change:** Anil Chakravarthy replaces long-tenured Shantanu Narayen; stock dropped on the news — markets tend to punish unexpected leadership transitions at large-cap tech companies.
- **Hormuz tanker attacks:** Two tankers hit on an overnight transit. WTI's muted reaction (-0.56%) suggests traders aren't yet pricing in disruption, but the Strait handles ~20% of global oil flows — worth watching for follow-through.
- **World food prices:** FAO reports highest levels since 2022 amid mounting supply risks — a potential secondary inflation input the Fed will need to weigh.
- **Xi's U.S. visit:** China's President Xi reportedly planning to bring a large CEO delegation on a U.S. visit — a trade-thaw signal that could shift tariff expectations if confirmed.
