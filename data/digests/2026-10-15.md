# Daily Market Brief — 2026-10-15

**TL;DR:** A CoreWeave debt crisis ignited a broad AI/tech rout while hot PPI data (wholesale prices +6.3% YoY, biggest gain since 2022) paradoxically pushed investors into Treasuries and defensives — the 10-year yield dropped 1.77% to 5.10% as money rotated hard out of growth.

---

## What Moved Markets

**The split tape:** Dow +0.97% and Russell 2000 +0.96% surged while Nasdaq fell -0.33% and QQQ dropped -0.63% — a textbook rotation from growth into value/small-cap.

**AI/Chip selloff:** CoreWeave's credit default swap spreads blew out, spotlighting the debt-heavy infrastructure model underlying the AI boom. Chip stocks broadly sold off, with Reuters estimating ~$2 trillion in market cap at risk. NVDA -2.05%, while AMD and Micron also fell.

**PPI shock:** September producer prices rose +6.3% annually, largest gain since 2022. Retail sales also beat. Economists flagged that Fed rate cuts are off the table near-term — yet the 10-year yield *fell* 9 bps to 5.10% as flight-to-safety demand from the tech rout overwhelmed inflation concerns.

**Commodities bid:** WTI crude +1.02% to $96.06 on Middle East risk (Houthi threats to Saudi Arabia, Hormuz shipping concerns, U.S. reportedly weighing use of "mother of all bombs" vs. Iran). Gold +1.66% to $4,176 — a dual safe-haven and inflation hedge play. Dollar softened -0.42% to 102.12.

**Bitcoin/Crypto:** BTC +1.05% to $82,421, ETH +0.91% — moving loosely with gold as an alternative store of value.
