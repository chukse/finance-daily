# Daily Market Brief — 2026-11-02

**TL;DR:** Oil's 7.7% crash on Iran peace-talk hopes dominated the session, splitting markets along a tech-weak/defensive-strong axis ahead of U.S. midterm elections.

---

## What Moved Markets

**Oil collapse** was the day's defining event. WTI fell $8.00 to $96.10, the sharpest single-day drop in months, as diplomatic progress on Iran eased supply-risk premiums. This dragged energy sector sentiment but flowed through as a disinflationary signal, pulling the 10-year Treasury yield down 3.5 bps to 4.894%.

**Tech sold off sharply.** Nasdaq fell 1.08%, underperforming everything else. The QQQ dropped 1.42%. A "crisis of confidence" in chip stocks per MarketWatch, plus Apple's earnings arriving after the close under new CEO John Ternus, kept large-cap tech under pressure.

**Dow outperformed (+0.50%)**, benefiting from its energy/industrial tilt where lower oil is a cost tailwind, and from rotation out of growth names.

**VIX surged 10.4% to 21.44** — midterm election uncertainty compounding tech earnings anxiety. Prediction markets now show ~75% odds of Democrats retaking the Senate.

**Gold rose 1.39% to $3,953**, classic haven bid with the VIX spike. Dollar was flat at 101.73.

**Bitcoin fell 1.91% to $76,754**, tracking risk-off in tech broadly.
