# Daily Market Brief — 2026-10-22

**TL;DR:** Meta's AI spending shock and Middle East oil escalation triggered a broad tech selloff, with Nasdaq down 2% and VIX spiking 13%.

---

## What Moved Markets

**AI spending backlash** was the dominant driver. Meta sank ~6% after heavy AI capex pushed free cash flow negative, rattling the entire growth complex. NVDA –3.8%, AMZN –3.7%, GOOGL –2.1%, MSFT –1.3%. QQQ fell 2.18% vs. the Dow's near-flat –0.06%, a classic growth-to-defensive rotation. VIX +12.9% to 18.83 confirms rising near-term anxiety.

**Oil and geopolitics** added pressure. Brent reportedly topped $111 (WTI +1.25% to $98.36) after Trump hinted at Iran action and the U.S. ordered staff to leave Saudi Arabia; Houthi strikes on Saudi targets further stoked risk. Airlines felt it immediately — American Airlines guided to a larger 2026 loss citing fuel costs.

**Rates and safe havens.** The 10Y yield fell sharply, –2.88% to 4.998%, as investors rotated into Treasuries. Gold moved the opposite direction (–1.98%), unusual but consistent with dollar-funded deleveraging. Bitcoin –2.9%, ETH –3.0%.

**France/Europe.** French sovereign risk premiums reached euro-zone crisis levels; Macron publicly clashed with Trump commentary. No direct US market impact yet, but worth watching.
