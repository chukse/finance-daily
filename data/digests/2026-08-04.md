# Daily Market Brief — 2026-08-04

**TL;DR:** AI-driven earnings wave lifts all major indices 1.3–2.1%, while Hormuz deal hopes crater oil 3%+ and push gold above $4,100.

---

## What Moved Markets

**Risk-on across the board.** S&P 500 +1.48% to 7,600, Nasdaq +2.13%, Russell 2000 +1.73%. VIX dropped to 15.59. Breadth was broad; this wasn't just a mega-cap squeeze.

**Earnings were the fuel.** Caterpillar surged ~9% on record quarterly revenue and a raised 2026 sales target, citing data-center construction demand — a proxy AI trade through industrial equipment. Palantir beat Q2 estimates handily. McDonald's and Pfizer also topped consensus. These prints extended the AI-infrastructure earnings validation theme that's driven summer gains.

**Hormuz diplomacy crushed oil.** WTI fell 3.17% to $77.79. Treasury Secretary Bessent said a deal to reopen the Strait of Hormuz could come Tuesday or Wednesday, explicitly framing it as a "last chance" warning to Iran. Qatar echoed the optimism. That removed a risk premium that had been embedded in crude for weeks.

**Gold surged despite the de-escalation signal.** GC +2.14% to $4,120, suggesting buyers are treating Iran uncertainty as unresolved and the dollar's flat performance (+0.01% to 99.97) as insufficient support for the greenback. The 10Y yield dipped modestly to 4.661%.

**Yen defense coordinated.** Bessent confirmed the U.S. backed Japan's yen intervention and is working with Tokyo to stabilize Asian FX. Dollar/yen implications are significant for exporters.
