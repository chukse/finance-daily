# Daily Market Brief — 2026-10-17

**TL;DR:** A 3.5% WTI crude spike and a 10-year yield touching 5.10% hammered tech-heavy indices, with Nasdaq off 2.15% and VIX surging 21% to nearly 20.

---

## What Moved Markets

**Rates & Oil the twin pressure points.** The 10-year Treasury yield hit 5.098%—a psychologically significant threshold—compressing equity multiples across the board. WTI crude jumped $3.44 to $101.41, its strongest close above $100 in months, adding stagflation anxiety to the rate pain. WTI's move is likely tied to Hurricane Trudy, Middle East/Russia-Ukraine supply uncertainty, and Trump's waiver of Russia sanctions under the Graham Act failing to ease supply concerns.

**Equities: growth sold hard, defensives held.** Nasdaq -2.15%, Russell 2000 -1.66%, S&P 500 -1.09%, Dow -0.34%. The Dow's relative resilience reflects a rotation away from duration-sensitive growth names. VIX at 20 signals the market is pricing in continued turbulence.

**Dollar softened slightly** (-0.16% to 101.84) despite rising yields—unusual divergence, possibly driven by safe-haven flows into gold (+0.26% to $4,181) and geopolitical hedging.

**Bitcoin flat** at $81,390, showing no strong safe-haven or risk-off directional signal today.
