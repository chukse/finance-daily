# Daily Market Brief — 2026-09-30

**TL;DR:** Softer-than-expected core PCE inflation (3.0% in August) fueled a tech-led rally, but the 10-year yield still climbed to 5.29% as oil surged 2.1% on Middle East risk.

## What Moved Markets

**The catalyst:** Core PCE came in at 3.0% in August, well below expectations, briefly tempering Fed rate-hike bets. S&P 500 +0.52%, Nasdaq +0.98%. The Dow lagged (-0.08%), dragged by energy-cost sensitivity and industrial names.

**Rates & oil cut against the soft-landing narrative:** The 10-year yield still pushed to 5.289% (+6.5 bps), a continued pressure point. WTI crude jumped to $91.27 (+2.1%) as Middle East risk premiums rebuild — Wall Street banks are raising their oil price forecasts. Hapag-Lloyd raised guidance again on strong freight rates, consistent with the WSJ's "soaring transport costs spreading through the economy" story.

**Dollar flat; gold muted:** DXY essentially unchanged at 101.41. Gold at $4,190 gained only 0.25% despite the inflation/geopolitical backdrop — a notable disconnect the market is debating.

**VIX:** Eased to 15.87, suggesting the soft PCE print was net reassuring despite the cross-currents.
