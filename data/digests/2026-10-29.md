# Daily Market Brief — 2026-10-29

**Daily Brief — October 29, 2026**

**TL;DR:** Alphabet cratered 12.8% on an AI-driven search revenue miss, dragging Nasdaq down 1.4% while small-caps rallied and the 10Y yield touched exactly 5.00%.

---

## What Moved Markets

**Alphabet earnings shock** dominated the session. GOOGL fell 12.8% — on pace for its largest-ever post-earnings drop — after Q3 results showed AI tools are measurably cannibalizing search ad revenue. The miss reignited broader AI monetization fears.

**Big Tech capex hesitation** compounded the pressure. Both Google and Microsoft signaled no near-term increase in capex forecasts, spooking investors who had priced in an accelerating buildout cycle. MSFT fell 1.0%, NVDA -0.9%.

**ServiceNow cratered 25%** after earnings revived concerns that AI is disrupting enterprise software demand rather than augmenting it — a second major AI disruption signal in one session.

**Caterpillar** shares dived after the company held its annual sales forecast flat despite cost pressures, disappointing bulls expecting upward revision.

**The divergence:** Russell 2000 +0.98% while Nasdaq -1.43% — classic rotation out of mega-cap tech into rate-sensitive small-caps as the 10Y yield dipped to 5.00% (-0.87 bps). VIX fell 2.3% to 16.5, suggesting the selloff was orderly.

**Commodities:** WTI crude slipped 1.4% to $101.39. Gold -0.4% to $4,034. Dollar index -0.2%.
