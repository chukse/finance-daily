# Daily Market Brief — 2026-07-24

**TL;DR:** Alphabet's -7% and Tesla's -15% earnings collapses dragged Nasdaq down 2.15% while new sweeping tariffs and elevated bond yields added macro pressure across risk assets.

---

## What Moved Markets

**Tech earnings rout** was the dominant force. Alphabet cratered 7.1% and Tesla collapsed 14.5% on results that disappointed — together they account for the bulk of the Nasdaq's 2.15% decline, the worst index performance of the session. AMZN also fell 4.6% in sympathy with the earnings-driven risk-off tone.

**New tariff wave.** Trump announced "sweeping" duties on 60 trade partners as prior global tariffs expired, framed around forced-labor justifications. Switzerland and others issued immediate rebukes. This adds a fresh layer of uncertainty for multinationals.

**Oil gave back gains.** WTI fell 3.0% to $89.39 despite earlier Mideast escalation (Iran strikes, Ukraine infrastructure attacks). The WSJ flagged the energy spike as likely temporary; the reversal pulled energy sector drag into positive territory for broader market math.

**Rates and the Fed.** The 10Y yield slipped 2.5 bps to 4.679% — still near January 2025 highs — as oil fell and the dollar was flat. Reuters flagged Fed Chair Warsh facing pressure as bond yields spike; WSJ ran a "Is the Fed About to Hike?" piece, keeping rate-hike risk in the conversation.

**VIX** ticked up only modestly to 18.8 — fear elevated but not panicked.
