# Daily Market Brief — 2026-08-20

**TL;DR:** Crypto surged on Trump's Clarity Act push, oil jumped on Iran threats, and gold held above $4,500 while equities drifted modestly higher.

---

## What moved markets

**Crypto:** Bitcoin +3.87% to $71,948, ETH +2.24%. Trump publicly urged Congress to pass the Clarity Act, followed by a positive White House meeting with crypto industry figures. Coinbase among premarket movers.

**Oil:** WTI +1.50% to $87.12. Trump's "Economic D-Day" threat against Iran and pledged secondary sanctions on Iran's backers drove the move.

**Gold:** +0.93% to $4,531. Holding firmly above $4,500 post-Fed minutes; JPMorgan flagged Treasury's buyback program as a structural support for the trade.

**Bonds:** 10Y yield fell 11 bps to 4.653% as Treasury yields "cooled from early highs." The move is notable given JPMorgan's concurrent warning that the buyback blitz could ultimately push yields *higher*.

**Equities:** Broad but thin gains — S&P +0.21%, Russell 2000 outperformed at +0.50%. VIX ticked up 2.69% to 15.29 despite the green tape, suggesting some hedging activity.
