# Daily Market Brief — 2026-10-27

**TL;DR:** AI momentum and easing oil prices lifted tech and small-caps; Boeing cratered on a delayed 737 Max ramp-up while the 10Y yield pushed toward 5%.

---

## What Moved Markets

**AI/Tech bid sustained.** Nasdaq +1.10%, Russell 2000 +1.05%, S&P +0.51% as chip stocks gained a second consecutive session. Nvidia +0.81%, AMZN +1.92%, GOOGL +1.46%. Catalysts: Oracle's ChatGPT/Codex integration announcement, Nvidia and AMD jointly committing $4B into Meta's AI infrastructure, and anticipation of Big Tech earnings. VIX fell 4.5% to 17.6, reflecting calmer sentiment.

**Boeing -9%.** Company disclosed it won't meaningfully ramp 737 Max production until late 2027, crushing free cash flow expectations. The Dow's flat close (-0.04%) largely reflects Boeing's drag offsetting broader gains.

**Oil eased, gold advanced.** WTI -1.76% to ~$100, pulled lower as Persian Gulf supply increases post-Iran War. Gold +0.89% to $4,074—elevated amid geopolitical residue and a 10Y yield pressing 5.0% (+1.8 bps to 4.99%).

**Rates and dollar firm.** 10Y at 4.994% keeps pressure on rate-sensitive sectors. Fed expected to hold this week but market pricing in further hikes; Kevin Warsh watch is active for hawkish signals.

**Consumer data supportive.** Conference Board consumer confidence improved in October; core capital goods orders and shipments rose in September on AI investment demand—consistent with the economy "gathering steam."
