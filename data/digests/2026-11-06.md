# Daily Market Brief — 2026-11-06

**Daily Brief — November 6, 2026**

**TL;DR:** Democratic midterm sweep reshapes macro expectations, sparking a tech-led rebound as the Nasdaq reclaims ground from correction territory; VIX drops sharply but stays elevated at 25.9.

---

## What Moved Markets

**Equities — partial recovery, uneven.** Nasdaq +1.01%, S&P 500 +0.46%, Russell 2000 +0.63%, Dow -0.12%. The split reflects rotation into growth/tech after the recent correction, with blue-chip industrials lagging. VIX -8.1% to 25.9 — fear easing but not gone.

**Macro catalyst — Democratic sweep.** A clean Democratic election win is being priced as a path to higher spending, potentially stickier inflation, and a more aggressive Fed. The 10Y yield sits at 4.827% (down just 2 bps today but still near cycle highs). Market rethinking the rate path: headlines explicitly flag a "blue wave" prompting interest rate reassessment.

**Jobs — strong beat.** October payrolls +165,000 vs. expectations; unemployment slipped to 4.1%. Solid labor market gives the Fed room to stay restrictive — consistent with the Bank of England hiking for the first time since the Iran war and the ECB holding at 2.5%.

**Dollar, gold, oil.** DXY +0.67% to 102.23 on higher-for-longer repricing. Gold -1.03% to $4,071 — typical inverse dollar move. WTI -0.78% to $89.81, drifting lower on demand concerns amid slowing eurozone growth (Q3 GDP +0.4%, France contracting).
