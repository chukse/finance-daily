# Daily Market Brief — 2026-09-26

**TL;DR:** Equities rallied broadly on a US-China tariff truce, MSFT surged on an $11.6B cloud deal, but the 10Y yield hit ~5.18% and oil slid on an inventory build.

---

## What Moved Markets

**Risk-on, but yields bite.** S&P 500 +0.51%, Dow leading at +0.93%, while small-caps (Russell 2000 +0.07%) lagged — consistent with rate sensitivity at the short end. VIX dropped 5.1% to 14.87, signaling reduced near-term fear.

**US-China deal.** Beijing confirmed a $30B mutual tariff cut and an AI dialogue framework during Xi's visit. The primary catalyst for the day's broad risk appetite; dollar slipped 0.32% to 100.97 on reduced safe-haven demand.

**10Y Treasury at 5.18%.** Highest in nearly two decades per CNBC. The move is compressing equity multiples, especially for rate-sensitive small-caps, and keeping mortgage and corporate borrowing costs elevated.

**Oil -2.33% to $92.41.** An unexpected build in US crude stockpiles drove the decline despite ongoing Strait of Hormuz disruption (DP World rerouting cargo). The inventory data outweighed geopolitical risk premium.

**Gold +0.54% to $4,321.** Holding near highs; benefiting from Middle East tensions (Trump rejected Iran's ceasefire proposal) and dollar weakness simultaneously.
