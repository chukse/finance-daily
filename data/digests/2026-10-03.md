# Daily Market Brief — 2026-10-03

**TL;DR:** Equities rallied broadly as tech led gains, but the 10Y yield pushing to 5.28% and a possible Fed hike keep rate risk front and center.

## What Moved Markets

**Equities up across the board.** S&P 500 +0.73% to 7,722, Nasdaq +1.19%, Russell 2000 +0.94% — broad participation. VIX dropped 6.6% to 15.31, signaling the session was orderly and risk appetite was intact.

**Rates remain the overhang.** 10Y Treasury yield rose another 7.6 bps to 5.28% — a level that historically pressures equity multiples. WSJ flags another Fed rate hike is "possible," with next week's jobs report as the key swing factor.

**Oil fell 1.9% to $91.11** despite a fire/smoke sighting near an Aramco facility in Riyadh — market apparently discounted the incident as non-supply-threatening. Gold slid 0.95% to $4,163, likely pressured by the higher real yield environment.

**Dollar slightly softer** (-0.17% to 101.93), giving modest tailwind to multinationals.
