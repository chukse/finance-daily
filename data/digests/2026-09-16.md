# Daily Market Brief — 2026-09-16

**TL;DR:** Markets drifted higher ahead of a Fed rate hike decision, with oil's 4% plunge on Saudi pipeline news and gold's surge to $4,394 stealing the session's volatility.

---

## What Moved Markets

**Oil/Gold divergence.** WTI crude fell $4.30 to $101.52 (-4.07%) after the U.S. confirmed a damaged Saudi pipeline will restart within days, easing supply fears. Gold moved the opposite direction, +1.40% to $4,393.50, as investors hedged ahead of the Fed.

**Fed hike in focus.** The FOMC is expected to raise rates for the first time in three years. The 10Y yield slipped 4 bps to 4.955%—just below the psychologically sensitive 5% level—suggesting markets have largely priced the move but remain cautious. Dan Niles publicly warned the 10Y is headed to 6%.

**Equities split.** Tech-heavy Nasdaq (+0.77%) and Russell 2000 (+0.48%) outperformed while the Dow (-0.06%) lagged. VIX fell 2.91% to 16.7, indicating near-term fear is cooling despite the macro overhang. The dollar was essentially flat at 99.67.

**Freight warning.** J.B. Hunt flagged Q3 cost pressures and shares sank 12%, dragging the trucking/logistics complex.
