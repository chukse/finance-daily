# Daily Market Brief — 2026-07-07

**TL;DR:** Tech/chip stocks rolled over on renewed AI competition fears while the Dow hit fresh records above 53,000, underscoring a sharp rotation from growth to value.

---

## What Moved Markets

**Rotation trade dominated.** Nasdaq fell 0.70% and QQQ dropped 1.28% while the Dow gained 0.23% (record territory) and Russell 2000 added 0.45% — a textbook large-cap growth-to-value/small-cap shift.

**Chip stocks were the epicenter.** Samsung reported a 19-fold profit surge but shares disappointed, reigniting fears that AI chip demand may be peaking or shifting. NVDA fell 1.48%; the move was amplified by a Reuters report that DeepSeek is pushing its own chip development, threatening Nvidia's dominance.

**Oil +1.69% to $69.71** on two converging catalysts: a report of an Iranian attack on commercial ships in the Strait of Hormuz, and Ukrainian drones striking Russia's largest oil refinery. Mideast tension also lifted the 10Y yield +0.36% to 4.50% and nudged gold up 0.58% to $4,179.

**Gold holding near $4,200** despite the dollar barely moving (+0.08%). Safe-haven demand from geopolitical noise is providing a floor.

**Bitcoin -1.54% to $63,010; ETH -1.75%.** Risk-off in crypto tracks the tech selloff; a Yahoo Finance piece flagging Japan's bond market as a threat to easy-money trades may be adding pressure.

## Stories Worth Knowing

**Beijing eyeing curbs on overseas access to Chinese AI models (Reuters exclusive).** If enacted, this limits DeepSeek's international reach but also escalates tech decoupling — a double-edged headline for U.S. chip and AI names.
