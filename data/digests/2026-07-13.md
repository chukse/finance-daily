# Daily Market Brief — 2026-07-13

**Daily Brief — Monday, July 13, 2026**

**TL;DR:** U.S. airstrikes on Iran rattled risk appetite — oil surged 3.2%, chips sold off hard, and the VIX jumped 9% as geopolitical tension overshadowed an otherwise quiet session.

---

## What Moved Markets

**Iran conflict dominates.** After the U.S. struck new Iranian targets following Tehran's refusal to open the Strait of Hormuz, WTI crude jumped $2.31 to $73.72. Trump's stated ambition to control the Strait and extract payment for U.S. naval protection added further uncertainty. Gold, surprisingly, fell 0.81% to $4,070 — likely profit-taking after prior safe-haven flows rather than reduced risk perception.

**Chips drag Nasdaq.** The SOX-adjacent trade cracked. NVDA fell 1.1% and SK Hynix's ADR plunged on leverage concerns tied to the geopolitical shock following a hot debut. QQQ dropped 1.47%, Nasdaq -0.85% — the worst index of the day.

**Divergence within equities.** The Dow eked out +0.17% while the S&P slid 0.31%, with the Russell 2000 down 0.49%. AAPL (+1.91%), AMZN (+1.10%), and MSFT (+1.00%) cushioned large-cap indices.

**Rates and dollar.** 10Y yield nudged up 1.6 bps to 4.585%. Dollar essentially flat at 100.95, suggesting no strong flight-to-dollar bid despite the Middle East escalation.

**VIX.** +9.05% to 16.39 — still moderate historically but the sharpest single-day spike in weeks. Oppenheimer flagged seasonal correction risk independently.
