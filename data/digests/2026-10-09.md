# Daily Market Brief — 2026-10-09

**Daily Brief — October 9, 2026**

**TL;DR:** Broad risk-on rally with tech leading, but the dominant story is SpaceX's wireless network ambitions crushing telecom stocks while gold hits $4,219 and the 10Y yield pushes deeper into 5.25% territory.

---

## What Moved Markets

**Equities broadly higher.** S&P 500 +0.53% to 7,807; Dow outperformed at +0.77%; Nasdaq +0.51%. VIX fell 3.4% to 14.88, signaling reduced near-term anxiety. Headlines cite a tech rebound following a prior session's sell-off, with earnings season approaching as a catalyst for positioning.

**Telecoms wrecked by SpaceX.** Verizon headed for its worst single-day decline since 2002 after SpaceX announced U.S. wireless network plans. AT&T and T-Mobile also fell; tower stocks like Crown Castle caught collateral damage. SpaceX's spectrum deal rattled the sector globally, with European telcos sliding in parallel.

**Rates and gold diverge — unusually.** 10Y Treasury yield rose 3.2 bps to 5.248%, a level that historically pressures equities and gold alike. Yet gold surged 1.5% to $4,219, suggesting safe-haven or inflation-hedge demand is overriding the yield headwind. Gold is on track for a weekly gain.

**Oil marginally softer despite supply threat.** WTI slipped $0.18 to $91.31 even as Hurricane Isaias disrupted Gulf of Mexico production and threatened refineries. Markets appear to be discounting the disruption as temporary.

**China-EU hybrid deal.** Beijing and Brussels agreed to halve Chinese hybrid vehicle exports to Europe — a significant trade concession that removes an escalation risk and likely supported sentiment in European auto-adjacent names.
