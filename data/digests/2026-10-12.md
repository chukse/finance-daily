# Daily Market Brief — 2026-10-12

**TL;DR:** Markets split on rising oil and geopolitical stress, with Dow eking out gains while tech dragged Nasdaq -0.44%; 10Y yield holds near 5.24% ahead of Fed minutes and bank earnings.

---

## What Moved Markets

**Oil & geopolitics dominated.** WTI crude jumped +1.48% to $93.21, driven by Russia strikes on Ukraine energy infrastructure and ongoing Middle East tension (Iran-backed militants pressuring Saudi Arabia, FAA warning airlines over Saudi airspace). The move pressured risk assets and lifted the dollar +0.38% to 102.60.

**Rate/macro backdrop remains tight.** The 10Y yield slipped fractionally to 5.24% but remains elevated. Fed's Hammack reiterated support for restrictive policy, saying higher rates may be needed to prevent an "inflationary mindset"—a hawkish signal ahead of this week's Fed minutes release.

**Rotation visible in index divergence.** Dow +0.38% (financials, energy, defensives) vs. Nasdaq -0.44% and Russell 2000 -0.45%. VIX ticked up +2.56% to 15.22—still low, but directionally notable.

**Gold fell -0.53% to $4,194** as the stronger dollar and rising oil crowded out safe-haven flows. Bitcoin -0.85% to $82,966, Ethereum -0.54%.
