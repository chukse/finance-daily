# Daily Market Brief — 2026-08-15

**Daily Brief — August 15, 2026**

**TL;DR:** Large-caps slipped modestly as the 10-year yield jumped 5.6 bps to 4.70% and Strait of Hormuz tensions lifted oil, while small-caps bucked the trend and VIX eased to 14.25.

---

## What Moved Markets

**Rates & macro:** The 10Y Treasury yield rose +1.19% to 4.696%, steepening the curve ahead of next week's Fed minutes. The dollar softened 0.29% to 99.67, partially offsetting the yield pressure on equities.

**Middle East / Oil:** Iran rejected Trump's Strait of Hormuz claims and another ship was reportedly struck in the region. WTI crude gained +1.42% to $82.40. Hormuz traffic is visibly slowing per Reuters; refiners were cited as a partial market tailwind per WSJ.

**Large-cap drag vs. small-cap divergence:** S&P 500 -0.17%, Nasdaq -0.28%, Dow -0.20% — but Russell 2000 outperformed at +0.51%, a classic risk-rotation signal favoring domestic cyclicals over rate-sensitive mega-caps.

**Inflation data:** WSJ flagged cooling inflation as a key story, supporting the softer VIX (-2.60% to 14.25) even as yields rose.

**Gold & Bitcoin:** Gold hit $4,380 (+0.38%), continuing its 2026 run. Bitcoin essentially flat at $62,945.
