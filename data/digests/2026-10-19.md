# Daily Market Brief — 2026-10-19

**TL;DR:** AI stocks rebounded sharply on dip-buying, lifting Nasdaq 1.19% and crushing the VIX 7.85%, while Amazon surged 5% on unspecified catalyst and the 10Y yield crept toward 5.15%.

---

## What Moved Markets

**Tech/AI rebound dominated.** Nasdaq +1.19% vs. Dow essentially flat (-0.02%), a classic growth-over-value rotation. VIX dropped to 18.4 from ~20, signaling the prior session's fear largely unwound. The move was explicitly framed as dip-buying in AI-linked names after a bruising recent selloff; CoreWeave received a Goldman upgrade.

**Rates stayed elevated.** The 10Y Treasury yield rose another 9 bps to 5.144% — a level that continues to create a ceiling for rate-sensitive equities and keep the Dow lagging. Dollar barely moved (+0.13%), suggesting the yield rise is more inflation/supply concern than safe-haven demand.

**Oil pulled back sharply.** WTI -2.16% to $99.22, giving back recent gains. WSJ noted futures pared losses from a Russia sanctions waiver — meaning the administration eased some Russian oil restrictions, adding supply expectations to the market.

**Gold slipped, Bitcoin flat.** Gold -0.48% to $4,161 despite elevated rates context; Bitcoin -0.20% at $81,852, essentially a non-event.
