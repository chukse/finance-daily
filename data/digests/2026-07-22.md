# Daily Market Brief — 2026-07-22

**Daily Brief — July 22, 2026**

**TL;DR:** Equities rallied broadly led by small-caps and tech, but rising oil prices on Hormuz tensions and looming Alphabet/Tesla earnings kept the session edgy.

---

## What Moved Markets

**Equities up across the board.** S&P 500 +0.89% to 7,509; Nasdaq +1.29%; Russell 2000 led at +1.53%, signaling risk-on breadth. VIX nudged higher to 17.45 (+2.35%) despite the rally — reflecting hedging ahead of major earnings tonight.

**Oil the session's shock.** WTI +2.17% to $86.75; Brent hovering near $95. Driver: renewed Red Sea/Strait of Hormuz disruptions after Secretary Rubio said Iran is not serious about talks and pledged to protect Hormuz shipping. More disrupted Red Sea shipments confirmed. Six-week high on crude.

**Gold extended its run** to $4,122.50 (+1.26%), reflecting geopolitical risk premium building alongside oil.

**10Y yield crept up** to 4.64% (+2.6 bps), modest but notable given equities' strength. Dollar essentially flat at 101.13.

**Bitcoin sold off** -1.36% to $65,599; ETH -0.53%. Crypto diverging from equity risk-on.

**AMD +/NVDA surge** driven by a confirmed WSJ report: AMD signing a major chips-and-investment deal with Anthropic worth up to $5 billion — a direct challenge to Nvidia's AI dominance. NVDA still gained +1.97% on broad AI enthusiasm.

**Super Micro Computer surged** on booming margins and orders, dragging Dell and HPE higher.
