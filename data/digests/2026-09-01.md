# Daily Market Brief — 2026-09-01

**TL;DR:** A global bond rout — led by Japan's 10-year yield hitting 3% and the US 10-year reaching 4.768%, its highest since January 2025 — combined with spiking oil prices to drag equities broadly lower on the first trading day of September.

---

## What Moved Markets

**Rates & Oil drove the selloff.** The 10-year Treasury yield rose to 4.768% (+2.1 bps) as oil (WTI +2.75% to $88.12) stoked fresh inflation fears, undermining the case for Fed cuts. Japan's 10-year crossing 3% amplified the global bond rout and pressured risk assets across the board.

**Iran headline added fuel to crude.** Trump vowing to hit Iran "hard" and Bessent signaling imminent Iranian bank sanctions pushed WTI to $88, a level that directly raises input cost concerns for manufacturers — August ISM manufacturing data already showed slowing activity with still-elevated input prices.

**Equities fell uniformly.** S&P 500 -0.44%, Nasdaq -0.71%, Russell 2000 -0.72%. Small caps and growth bore the brunt, consistent with a yield-driven selloff. VIX +3.4% to 15.43 — elevated but not alarmed.

**Dollar firmed, gold dipped.** DXY +0.20% to 99.62; gold -0.42% to $4,412. Bitcoin -1.0% to $77,767, with crypto miners (IREN -4%, TeraWulf -4%, Cipher Mining -6%) hit hardest as rising yields reverse rate-sensitive trades.
