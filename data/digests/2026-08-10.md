# Daily Market Brief — 2026-08-10

**TL;DR:** Equities rallied broadly on a Strait of Hormuz deal in progress and strong AI-driven earnings momentum, with tech and small-caps leading.

---

## What Moved Markets

**Hormuz situation dominating macro:** Shipping anxiety around the Strait of Hormuz is the day's central theme. European TTF gas surged 5% on supply concerns; WTI crude jumped 1.84% to $79.62. S&P 500 futures were cautious early but the index finished +0.62% as a deal appeared to be taking shape, per Yahoo Finance's headline of the S&P hitting a record high as Iran nuclear talks progressed.

**Rates & dollar:** 10Y Treasury yield dipped 2.1 bps to 4.66%, a mild risk-on/flight-to-safety hybrid signal. Dollar index barely moved (+0.13% to 99.73). Gold hit $4,392 (+1.18%), reinforcing geopolitical hedging.

**Nasdaq outperformance:** Nasdaq +1.30%, Russell 2000 +1.10%—both well ahead of Dow (+0.28%), signaling growth and risk appetite. VIX ticked up 3.69% to 15.45, a modest contradiction suggesting some residual hedging despite the rally.

**TSMC catalyst:** July 2026 revenue jumped 44.7% YoY on AI chip demand, providing a fundamental anchor for the tech move.
