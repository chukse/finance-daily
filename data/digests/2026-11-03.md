# Daily Market Brief — 2026-11-03

**TL;DR:** Tech-led rally ahead of 2026 midterm results pushed Nasdaq +1.13% even as Apple dragged and the 10-year yield crept toward 5%.

---

## What Moved Markets

**Tech/mega-cap divergence drove the day.** Nasdaq outpaced the S&P 500 (+0.64%) and Russell 2000 (+0.77%) while the Dow slipped -0.22%, reflecting rotation into growth names and away from the industrials-heavy blue chips. VIX fell to 20.52 (-2.98%), suggesting markets are not pricing a chaotic midterm outcome.

**Rates remain the tension point.** The 10-year yield rose to 4.952% (+1.19%), holding just under the psychologically significant 5% level. The dollar was essentially flat (101.79), so there's no currency amplification yet.

**Oil eased on Iran diplomacy hopes.** WTI fell to $96.05 (-0.43%) for a second consecutive day as the U.S. signaled renewed diplomatic engagement with Iran. Saudi Arabia's economy shrank 3.8% as OPEC output cuts weigh on its own GDP.

**Gold pulled back** to $3,941 (-0.71%), consistent with a mild risk-on tone and modest dollar stability.

**Bitcoin and Ethereum both gained** (+1.33% and +2.17% respectively), partly following broader risk appetite, despite a headline that hackers stole $6.3B in crypto via fake AI job interviews targeting Binance.

**OpenAI raised less than half its $30B target**, a notable signal of tightening private-market appetite for AI deals at current valuations.
