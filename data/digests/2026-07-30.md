# Daily Market Brief — 2026-07-30

**TL;DR:** Equity selloff deepened on a weak GDP print and Meta's earnings miss, while gold surged past $4,100 as the Fed held rates and inflation data offered only partial relief.

---

## What Moved Markets

**Macro:** Q2 GDP came in at 1.5% — below consensus — with June core PCE at 3.3%. The Fed held rates steady, but the "pause" is being read as a credibility problem rather than a pivot signal. 10Y yields rose 1.23% to 4.68%, pressuring equities across the board.

**Equities:** All major indices fell hard. The Dow led losses at -2.19%; Nasdaq dropped -1.74%. Despite the VIX falling 7.7% to 19.07, that reflects post-earnings volatility settling rather than genuine calm — the market absorbed significant single-stock damage.

**Gold:** +2.35% to $4,129, breaching $4,100 on a rate-hold + sticky inflation combination. Classic stagflation hedge bid.

**Oil:** WTI essentially flat at $84.35 despite Shell reporting near-record profits tied to Iran war-related supply disruption. Geopolitical risk is priced in.

**Crypto:** BTC +1.4% to $64,800; ETH +0.83%. Mild risk-off rotation into crypto as dollar stayed soft.
