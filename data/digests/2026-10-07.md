# Daily Market Brief — 2026-10-07

**Daily Brief — Wednesday, October 7, 2026**

**TL;DR:** The 10-year Treasury yield hitting 5.28% dragged equities lower across the board, with small-caps and crypto taking the hardest hits.

---

## What Moved Markets

**Rates dominated.** The 10-year yield climbed another 2.5 bps to 5.282%, a level not seen in roughly 24 years, keeping pressure on risk assets. The move lifted the dollar +0.44% to 102.27, which in turn weighed on gold (-1.17% to $4,139) and commodities broadly. Inflation expectations are contributing: a Fed consumer survey showed the one-year inflation outlook hit its highest reading since May 2023.

**Equities sold off, small-caps worst.** The Russell 2000 dropped -1.14%, the most rate-sensitive major index. The Dow fell -0.61%, Nasdaq -0.41%, S&P -0.25%. VIX edged up to 15.15 but remains contained — this is a yield-driven grind, not a panic.

**Oil fell despite geopolitical noise.** WTI dropped -1.39% to $88.20 as the IEA announced plans to release 100 million additional barrels from reserves, offsetting Middle East tension and a Gulf storm (Tropical Storm Isaias, forecast to hit the Gulf Coast as a hurricane Saturday).

**Crypto hit hard.** Bitcoin -2.84% to $83,128; Ethereum -5.54% to $2,548. The dollar/yield combo is the likely culprit, with ETH's sharper drop suggesting rotation out of altcoins specifically.
