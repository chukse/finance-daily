# Daily Market Brief — 2026-10-26

**TL;DR:** Anthropic's IPO filing and AI enthusiasm drove a broad tech rally; S&P +0.79%, Nasdaq +1.03%, VIX down 6.7%.

## What Moved Markets

**AI/Tech catalyst:** Anthropic's S-1 filing—showing explosive revenue growth despite massive compute costs and DOD contract risks—ignited AI sentiment broadly. Nasdaq led, QQQ +1.31%. Meta's reported $95B equity raise to fund AI expansion added fuel.

**Volatility compressed:** VIX fell to 18.15 (-6.68%), reflecting risk-on appetite and fading near-term uncertainty ahead of an expected Fed hold in October.

**Rates steady:** 10Y yield edged down 1.2 bps to 4.982%—negligible move; bond market largely sidelined as Fed pause is consensus.

**Oil lower:** WTI slid 1.1% to $102.09 as Saudi Arabia's defense minister met Iran's president in Tehran in a de-escalation move. Still above $100 on underlying Middle East risk premium.

**Gold firm:** +0.85% to $4,042, holding near record territory on persistent macro uncertainty.

**Brazil:** Markets crashed on Lula's surprise runoff win, which had not been priced in after prediction markets favored Bolsonaro.
