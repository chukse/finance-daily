# Daily Market Brief — 2026-07-15

**TL;DR:** Softer-than-expected wholesale prices (PPI -0.3% in June) reinforced disinflation momentum, pulling the VIX down 5.65% and the 10Y yield to 4.579%, while PayPal surged ~20% premarket on a reported $53B Stripe/Advent takeover bid.

---

## What Moved Markets

**Rates & macro:** June PPI fell 0.3% — the first monthly decline in nearly a year — driven by gasoline. NY Fed's Williams confirmed inflation has peaked and rates are "well positioned," cementing market expectations that the Fed is done hiking. 10Y yield slid to 4.579% (-0.65%); dollar dipped 0.40% to 100.88. VIX dropped to 16.19.

**Equity indices:** Price data unavailable for all major indices (S&P 500, Nasdaq, Dow, Russell 2000). Premarket tone was constructive given the macro backdrop and earnings beats.

**Earnings — big banks/financials:** Morgan Stanley posted record quarterly revenue and profit; equities trading surged 69%. BlackRock's AUM hit a record $15 trillion with profits jumping. Combined with earlier bank reports, the headline is a clean sweep for big-bank earnings season.

**PayPal:** +~20% premarket on a WSJ report that Stripe and Advent are exploring a ~$53B takeover offer. Some analysts flagged the bid as potentially below fair value.

**AI/chips:** ASML beat on orders for advanced lithography equipment. UBS flagged continued momentum in an unnamed AI chipmaker that has doubled in three months. Morgan Stanley's record results were explicitly attributed to AI stock frenzy-driven trading and advisory volumes. Alibaba ADRs +4% on news Qwen AI will be integrated into Apple Intelligence.

**Geopolitics/oil:** The U.S. launched a fresh wave of strikes on Iran. WTI crude rose 0.50% to $79.74, with silver and gold relatively subdued (gold +0.17% to $4,068).

**Crypto:** Bitcoin +0.15% to $65,053; Ethereum +1.38% to $1,916, both credited in part to the soft CPI/PPI prints.
