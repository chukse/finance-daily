# Daily Market Brief — 2026-08-08

**TL;DR:** Risk-on session led by Nasdaq (+1.30%) and small caps (+1.10%) as a weak July jobs report reignited rate-cut hopes, gold hit a record $4,341, and options flow turbocharged the S&P 500's weekly surge.

---

## What Moved Markets

**Macro catalyst — surprise July job losses:** The US shed jobs unexpectedly in July, markets immediately dialed back rate-hike expectations, sending the 10Y yield down 1 bp to 4.66% and the dollar index sliding 0.37% to 99.60. That backdrop fueled the broad risk rally.

**Equities:** Nasdaq outperformed (+1.30%) with growth and small caps (Russell +1.10%) leading. VIX fell to 14.90, near its 2026 low. CNBC flagged record-breaking options activity as a mechanical amplifier of the S&P 500's weekly move.

**Gold surged to a record $4,341 (+2.33%):** Driven by dollar weakness, softer rate expectations, and persistent geopolitical risk — Iran/Hormuz uncertainty and the Ukraine war both active. WTI crude +1.15% to $78.18 on the same Hormuz concerns despite a deal reportedly close.

**Senate passed a short-term funding bill**, averting a federal shutdown and removing one near-term political risk overhang.

**Dollar/Yen:** Dollar weakened against the yen; WSJ notes no confirmed BoJ intervention.
