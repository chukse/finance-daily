# Daily Market Brief — 2026-09-28

**TL;DR:** Bonds sold off again as the 10-year yield hit 5.24%, dragging equities broadly lower and crushing gold by 3.6%.

## What moved markets

**Rates leading the damage.** The 10-year Treasury yield rose another 5 bps to 5.236%—a continuation of its march to multi-year highs—tightening financial conditions and pressuring equity valuations. VIX jumped 6.9% to 15.9, signaling rising near-term anxiety.

**Equities off across the board.** S&P 500 −0.59%, Nasdaq −0.67%, Dow −0.52%, Russell 2000 −0.41%. The selloff was broad but not panicked. Nearly half of S&P 500 components are already diverging from index direction, suggesting internal deterioration beneath the headline.

**Gold hit hard.** Fell 3.61% to $4,165—a notable reversal given geopolitical tension. Likely a combination of real yield pressure from the 10-year move and profit-taking.

**Oil flat despite Iran headlines.** WTI essentially unchanged at $92.45. Saudi pipeline reportedly ramping back up after earlier disruption, offsetting Iran war-risk premium. The move in oil is a non-event for now.

**Dollar steady.** DX +0.21% to 101.18; not adding significant pressure on its own.

**US-China tariff partial deal.** $60 billion in cuts agreed on consumer goods (dolls, fireworks). Rare earths—the strategically critical category—remain unresolved.
