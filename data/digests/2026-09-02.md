# Daily Market Brief — 2026-09-02

**TL;DR:** Equities rallied broadly on NVDA-led AI momentum and a Google ad-tech legal win, even as US-Iran Hormuz tensions pushed gold to $4,424 and a weak ADP jobs print (+38K vs. ~150K expected) flashed a soft-labor warning.

---

## What Moved Markets

**Risk-on, broad-based:** S&P 500 +0.53%, Russell 2000 outperformed at +0.96%, VIX dropped 4.83% to 15.55 — small-caps leading suggests genuine risk appetite rather than just mega-cap flows.

**AI/tech the primary driver:** NVDA +4.29% after Dell's strong quarterly beat confirmed ongoing AI infrastructure demand. NVDA-Equinix AI inference partnership announced. Berkshire's Abel called Alphabet a "significant player" in AI after Q2 stake increase.

**Google ad-tech ruling:** Court rejected the DOJ's push to force Google to divest its ad tech business. Modest positive for GOOGL (+0.44%) but removes a large overhang.

**Hormuz tensions lifted gold and oil:** Iran confirmed two tankers struck naval mines after US strikes; Energy Secretary Wright noted 17M barrels/day transit Hormuz. Gold surged +1.75% to $4,424; WTI +0.33% to $90.52. The geopolitical risk premium is real but equities shrugged it off.

**Weak jobs data ignored:** ADP private payrolls rose only 38,000 in August — well below ~150K consensus. Markets treated it as a soft-landing / rate-cut catalyst rather than a recession signal. 10Y yield held flat at 4.796%.

**Dollar soft:** DX -0.15% to 99.52, supporting gold and commodities.
