# Daily Market Brief — 2026-08-13

**TL;DR:** Soft inflation data and an NVDA-led AI surge pushed equities broadly higher, while WTI crude sank 2% on demand concerns and gold hit $4,443—up ~10% month-over-month.

---

## What Moved Markets

**Equities — mixed by index, tech leads.** S&P 500 +0.26%, Nasdaq +0.54%, Russell 2000 +0.61% (risk-on breadth), Dow -0.04% dragged by MSFT and AMZN. Subdued wholesale inflation data (PPI awaited) kept rate-hike fears contained; 10Y yield slipped to 4.68%.

**Oil — sharp drop.** WTI fell 2% to $81.57 on weaker demand outlook and rising U.S. crude inventories, outweighing Middle East tension (Hormuz traffic declining, U.S. Navy firing on a vessel breaking Iran's blockade). Dollar slipped 0.07% to 99.94, consistent with easing macro pressure.

**Gold — new high territory.** $4,443, +0.77% on the day, +~10% over the past month. Safe-haven bid from Iran war risk and dollar softness.

**AI names — bifurcated earnings reaction.** Nebius, Lumentum, and CoreWeave rallied hard on AI infrastructure demand. Cisco beat estimates but shares fell on margin concerns. Coherent also sold off post-earnings.

**Goldman Sachs** agreed to acquire ETF manager NEOS for $2.25B, the session's largest M&A headline.
