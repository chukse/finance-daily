# Daily Market Brief — 2026-10-06

**TL;DR:** S&P 500 and Nasdaq hit fresh record highs as the 10-year yield pulled back 3-4 bps and oil slipped, relieving twin pressures that had weighed on equities.

## What Moved Markets

**Equities — large-cap rally, small-caps lag**
S&P 500 +0.60% to 7,820; Nasdaq +0.50%; Dow +0.43%. Russell 2000 slipped -0.38%, underperforming as rate sensitivity kept smaller names in check. VIX fell to 15.1 (-2.51%), consistent with a risk-on tone.

**Rates — brief reprieve at an elevated level**
10-year yield eased to 5.277% (-0.64%), halting a recent selloff. PIMCO's senior advisor publicly called Treasuries "screaming good value" at current levels. The yield remains historically elevated and is the key macro tension point.

**Oil & dollar**
WTI +0.18% to $89.59 — essentially flat, but earlier intraday weakness in crude was cited as a catalyst for the yield pullback and equity lift. DXY softened -0.29% to 101.88, providing mild tailwind for risk assets.

**Gold**
Gold surged +0.84% to $4,192 — continuing its run as rates paused and geopolitical risks (Hormuz tanker threats, Iran drone plot near UK airbase) stayed elevated.

**Trade deficit**
August deficit widened to $105.6B, the largest since just before Trump's 2025 tariffs, signaling import front-running ahead of anticipated policy moves. No immediate market reaction.
