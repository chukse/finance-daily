# Daily Market Brief — 2026-09-21

**TL;DR:** Oil's 8.8% crash and a 10Y yield drop to 4.96% unlocked a broad risk-on rally, lifting the S&P 500 +1.36% and Nasdaq +1.99%, with Bitcoin surging 6% above $86K.

---

## What Moved Markets

**Oil collapse drives the session.** WTI crude fell $8.84 to $91.47 — one of the largest single-day drops in months — easing inflation and margin pressure across the economy. The 10Y Treasury yield fell 4 bps to 4.957%, and global yields followed, pulling the UK market higher simultaneously.

**AI stocks led the equity surge.** The Nasdaq outperformed on strength in semiconductors and AI-adjacent names. QQQ +2.41% vs. SPY +1.41% reflects the quality of the rotation — large-cap tech, not defensive or small-cap. Russell 2000 lagged at +0.58%.

**Bitcoin broke out.** BTC +6.06% to $86,056, clearing a key 50-week moving average analysts cite as a bull-market signal. ETH gained +4.29% to $2,756. Crypto appears to be catching a separate bid alongside risk assets.

**Gold slipped** -1.01% to $4,380 despite the dollar being nearly flat (+0.18%), suggesting the oil-driven inflation relief reduced safe-haven demand.

**VIX rose slightly** (+1.55% to 15.0) — modest and not alarming, likely options activity around the strong move rather than fear.
