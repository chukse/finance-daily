# Daily Market Brief — 2026-07-25

**Daily Brief — July 25, 2026**

**TL;DR:** Apple surged 3.5% on what appears to be earnings strength, powering the Dow while dragging the Nasdaq's losses narrower; oil tumbled 3.1% on demand fears as Middle East tensions escalated but failed to support prices.

---

## What Moved Markets

**Rotation, not rally.** The S&P 500 was essentially flat (+0.05%) as a sharp tech split masked underlying divergence: the Dow gained 0.46% while the Nasdaq fell 0.64% and the Russell 2000 slid 0.35%. VIX at 18.6 suggests no panic, but the small-cap and growth underperformance points to mild risk-off beneath the surface.

**Oil dropped hard.** WTI fell $2.88 to $89.31 despite Saudi military strikes on Houthi targets in Yemen and fresh America-Iran escalation (WSJ). Markets are pricing demand destruction over supply disruption—WSJ noted the oil rebound is already threatening GOP midterm positioning, implying elevated levels prior to today's drop.

**Rates eased slightly.** The 10Y yield dipped 2.4 bps to 4.679%, providing modest support for equities. The Fed meeting this week is apparently being run with unusual opacity under Chair Warsh, per MarketWatch.

**Gold held near record highs** at $4,067, up 0.52%, consistent with ongoing geopolitical stress (Middle East, Ukraine drone strike on a Russian refinery in Tyumen).

**Samsung/SK Hynix mega-deals.** South Korea's two chip giants announced a combined ~$950B in partnerships with US big tech, including a $200B Broadcom-Samsung AI chip foundry deal and Nvidia locking in SK Hynix memory supply as part of a $500B AI commitment. These are multi-year framework figures, not single transactions.
