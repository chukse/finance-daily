# Daily Market Brief — 2026-10-02

**Daily Brief — Friday, October 2, 2026**

**TL;DR:** A much weaker-than-expected September jobs report (only +29K, unemployment 4.2%) sent stocks to record highs by cooling rate-hike fears, even as the 10-year yield stubbornly nudged higher to 5.27%.

---

## What Moved Markets

**Jobs shock → equity rally.** September payrolls came in at just 29,000—well below any consensus estimate—and unemployment ticked up to 4.2%. Markets read this as reducing the Fed's room to hike further. S&P 500 +0.71%, Nasdaq +1.18% to a record close, Russell 2000 +0.92%. VIX dropped 4.3% to 15.68.

**Bond market not buying the pivot story.** Despite the soft jobs data, the 10-year yield added 5.5 bps to 5.27%—a notable divergence from equities. The WSJ flagged that Treasury yields are resuming their upward trend; the bond rout also hit the UK's FTSE 100, which posted its biggest weekly drop since April.

**Oil fell sharply; G7 releases reserves.** WTI dropped 1.6% to $91.39 after G7 nations agreed to release 100 million barrels of diesel and crude through the IEA—reportedly under U.S. pressure ahead of midterms. Brent pulled back below $100. Gold slipped 0.78% to $4,170.

**Dollar flat.** DX -0.15% to 101.95; no major move on the jobs number.
