# Daily Brief — September 3, 2026

**TL;DR:** Fed's Waller signals a September rate hold, Treasury yields drop 8bps, and risk assets rally broadly — gold and Bitcoin surge on a weakening dollar as Iran-Kuwait tensions spike oil above $92.

## What Moved Markets

**The macro driver:** Fed Governor Waller explicitly backed holding rates steady at the September meeting. The 10Y yield fell to 4.756% (-4bp), the dollar slid 0.54% to 99.02, and equities rallied across the board. VIX dropped to 14.95.

**Equities:** S&P +0.76%, Nasdaq +1.04%, Dow +0.95%. Small-caps lagged sharply — Russell 2000 barely moved (+0.07%), suggesting the rally is concentrated in large-cap growth.

**Nvidia/Hugging Face:** Nvidia confirmed a **$12.9B acquisition** of Hugging Face, the dominant open-weight AI model platform. Hugging Face's CEO told CNBC the company approached Jensen Huang directly weeks before the deal. NVDA gained a modest +0.72% — market had partially priced it.

**Tesla:** +6.77%, the session's standout large-cap mover. No single clear catalyst in headlines; likely short-covering or options activity.

**Geopolitics/Oil:** Iran struck Kuwait. Brent briefly topped **$96/barrel**; WTI settled at $92.09 (+1.19%). The move didn't dent bonds or equities — Waller's signal dominated.

**Gold & Bitcoin:** Gold surged **+3.74% to $4,529** — the Netherlands moving gold from New York to London "citing geopolitical unrest" underscored safe-haven demand. Bitcoin +4.36% to $80,673; ETH +4.33%. Bitcoin-gold correlation reportedly at a six-year high, per Yahoo Finance.

**Snowflake:** Beat earnings; AI product strategy described as "jelling." Stock surged intraday (specific close not listed but flagged as a major mover).
