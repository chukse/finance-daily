# Daily Market Brief — 2026-10-24

**Daily Brief — October 24, 2026**

**TL;DR:** Oil surged 3.8% on Persian Gulf supply tensions and Iran war fallout, dragging rate-sensitive equities lower as the 10-year yield pushed to 4.99%.

---

## What Moved Markets

**Rates & Dollar**
The 10-year Treasury yield climbed 3.5 bps to 4.988%, within a whisker of the psychologically significant 5% level. The dollar added 0.20% (DXY 101.81). Higher-for-longer fears amplified equity weakness.

**Oil**
WTI crude jumped $3.80 (+3.82%) to $103.27. The catalyst is clearly geopolitical: headlines confirm Persian Gulf oil flows have recovered post-Iran war, but ongoing tensions — Houthi ballistic missiles targeting Saudi cities, USS George Washington repositioning for potential Iran strikes — are keeping a substantial risk premium in the market.

**Equities**
The Dow underperformed sharply (-1.23% vs. S&P -0.52%), reflecting its rate-sensitivity and energy-cost exposure. Nasdaq held up relatively well (-0.21%), with QQQ nearly flat (-0.01%). VIX rose 2.2% to 19.45 — elevated but not panicked. Small caps (Russell 2000 -0.29%) were resilient relative to large-cap value.

**Gold & Crypto**
Gold fell $52 (-1.29%) to $4,008, an unusual decline during geopolitical stress — likely pressured by the rising real yield. BTC slipped 1.2% to $80,107; ETH underperformed at -1.78%.
