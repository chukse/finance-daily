# Daily Market Brief — 2026-09-17

**TL;DR:** Markets staged a broad post-Fed recovery as falling oil prices and easing yields pulled risk assets higher, with Nasdaq leading at +1.59%.

---

## What Moved Markets

**Fed aftermath reversal.** Stocks retraced Wednesday's post-Fed selloff, with the VIX dropping a sharp 10.45% to 15.86, signaling the panic faded fast. The catalyst: WTI crude fell $1.84 to $100.59 after Saudi Arabia reportedly offered additional supply through Hormuz following a pipeline attack, which in turn pulled the 10Y yield down 6 bps to 4.95%.

**Yields and oil doing the heavy lifting.** Lower crude reduces inflation expectations at the margin; lower yields ease pressure on growth valuations. That combination explains why Nasdaq (+1.59%) outpaced the Dow (+0.64%) and why small-caps (Russell 2000 +1.05%) kept pace with the S&P.

**Chip stocks the standout sector.** Micron, Intel, and peers extended a notable comeback per MarketWatch. NVDA +2.41% led the watchlist gainers in absolute percentage terms among mega-caps.

**Bank of England hawkish pivot.** BOE shifted tone toward rate hikes with UK inflation projected to breach 4%, reinforcing a divergence narrative: Fed on hold/cutting, BOE tightening. Dollar barely moved (-0.11% to 100.20).

## Stories Worth Knowing

**Oil supply relief, not demand collapse.** The crude drop is supply-driven (Saudi Hormuz offer post-pipeline attack), not a recession signal. Ryanair is already cutting winter flights preemptively citing oil cost risk.

**SEC tokenized stock exemption.** The securities regulator issued a five-year exemption for tokenized stock trading — a structural development for crypto/TradFi convergence that flew under the radar.

**CoreWeave $3B convertible debt sale.** The AI cloud infrastructure firm is raising significant capital, reflecting continued heavy investment demand in AI compute buildout.

**Novartis/Ionis cholesterol drug fails Phase 3.** A late-stage trial miss — relevant for biopharma watchers tracking the crowded lipid-lowering pipeline.

**Burry's NVDA/Palantir puts.** Pre-fund-closure disclosures showed ~$1.1B in put options against both names. Positions are now closed, but timing relative to NVDA's current levels makes for pointed reading.

**Trump-EU tariff threat.** Trump warned the EU over its "associate member" proposal for Canada, adding another trade friction point to monitor.
