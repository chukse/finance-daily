## What Moved Markets

**Broad selloff, tech hardest hit.** Nasdaq fell 1.33%, Russell 2000 dropped 1.30%, S&P 500 lost 0.69%, while the Dow held relatively firm at -0.22%. VIX edged down slightly to 15.75, suggesting the move was orderly rather than panicked.

**Gold surged, oil flat.** Gold +1.19% to $4,418, continuing its safe-haven bid amid Middle East tensions (UAE severed trade with Iran after a reported missile strike). WTI barely moved at $84.90.

**AI chip jitters.** NVDA's -2.34% drop drove broader tech weakness. Reporting flagged renewed U.S. efforts to close China chip export loopholes, adding regulatory overhang to the sector.

**Retail earnings mixed.** Target beat and raised guidance, aided by a large tariff refund, but shares still fell on execution concerns. Lowe's gave a cautious outlook citing "pressure" in DIY spending, sending its stock lower.
