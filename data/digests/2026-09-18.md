# Daily Market Brief — 2026-09-18

**Daily Brief — September 18, 2026**

**TL;DR:** Warren Buffett steps down as Berkshire chairman while rising Treasury yields (flirting with 5%) and a sharp oil drop pulled small-caps and the Dow lower; Bitcoin surged 5.7%.

---

## What Moved Markets

**Rates & Dollar pressure equities.** The 10Y Treasury yield climbed to 4.994%, up 5 bps, nearly touching the psychologically significant 5% level. WSJ cites markets pricing a more hawkish Fed path. The dollar index edged up 0.2% to 100.4. Small-caps bore the brunt — Russell 2000 -0.75% — consistent with rate-sensitive names repricing. S&P 500 ended -0.11%; Dow -0.42%. VIX eased to 15.3.

**Oil sold off hard.** WTI crude dropped $4.96 (-4.86%) to $96.96, a third consecutive down day as Saudi pipeline disruption fears partially eased. Diesel prices, however, hit a record on persistent supply issues. Gold was essentially flat at $4,398.

**Bitcoin ripped.** BTC +5.74% to $80,793; ETH +5.51% to $2,582. No single catalyst in headlines — likely a combination of dollar softness at the margin and risk appetite rotating away from rate-sensitive assets into crypto.

**BOJ rate rise rattles FX.** The yen sold off after a BOJ hike, flagged as a concern for Treasury Secretary Bessent — adding another variable to the rates complex.

## Stories Worth Knowing

- **Buffett exits.** Warren Buffett stepped down as Berkshire Hathaway chairman; son Howard Buffett succeeds him. With the 10Y near 5%, Buffett's long-stated rule — equities lose their appeal above that threshold — is getting fresh attention.

- **Fed SVB report.** A new Fed report pins blame for Silicon Valley Bank's 2023 collapse squarely on the Fed's own supervisors, who staff "should have known" the bank was vulnerable. Enforcement actions against former employees at Northstar, Amex, and Regions also released today.

- **OpenAI/Microsoft tension.** Microsoft AI CEO Mustafa Suleyman called OpenAI's latest revelation a "serious situation" on CNBC — vague but notable given the stakes of the partnership.

- **BOJ hike + German gas.** BOJ's rate increase is driving yen weakness and complicating U.S. Treasury dynamics. Separately, Putin envoys and Germany's AfD are reportedly in talks to restore Russian gas flows — a geopolitical wildcard for European energy heading into winter; Macron has called a G7 energy crisis meeting.

- **Oil outlook.** WSJ flags WTI could remain in triple digits well into 2027 despite today's pullback — diesel at record highs underscores the supply tightness underneath.
