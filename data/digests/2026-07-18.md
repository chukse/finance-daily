# Daily Market Brief — 2026-07-18

**TL;DR:** Chinese AI model Kimi K3 reignited chip-sector fears, dragging Nasdaq down 1.4% while a WTI crude spike of 4.5% on Iran strike escalation added to the risk-off tone.

---

## What Moved Markets

**Equities sold off broadly**, led by tech. S&P 500 -1.01%, Nasdaq -1.40%, Dow -0.77%, Russell 2000 -0.42%. VIX jumped 12.2% to 18.77, signaling a meaningful uptick in near-term fear.

**Chinese AI again rattled chips.** Moonshot AI's Kimi K3 model joined a string of capable, low-cost Chinese open-weight releases that are forcing Wall Street to reprice the AI infrastructure buildout thesis. Semiconductor names led the selloff.

**Crude surged 4.5% to $82.49 (WTI)** after the U.S. military confirmed its seventh consecutive night of strikes on Iran, with fresh disruptions to regional shipping. That's the clearest single driver of the oil move.

**Treasuries caught a modest bid** — 10Y yield slipped 4 bps to 4.54% — consistent with risk-off flows but not a full flight-to-safety. Dollar was flat at 100.75.

**Gold held above $4,000**, settling at $4,012.70 (+0.68%), supported by geopolitical uncertainty. Bitcoin largely sat out the session at $64,051 (+0.24%).
