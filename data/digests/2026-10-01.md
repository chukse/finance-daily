# Daily Market Brief — 2026-10-01

**TL;DR:** 10-year Treasury yields hit a 24-year high before pulling back, keeping equity gains muted while Accenture's blowout quarter sent IT services stocks sharply higher.

---

## What Moved Markets

**Rates & Dollar** — The 10Y yield touched its highest level since 2002 before ending the session down 1.30% to 5.224%. The move—driven by sticky factory-gate inflation and historically low layoffs—weighed on risk appetite globally, hit UK gilts (highest since 1998), and dragged the FTSE to a three-month low. The dollar gained 0.72% to 102.18 on the yield spike.

**Equities** — Indexes barely budged: S&P +0.18%, Nasdaq +0.22%, Dow flat. Small-caps (Russell 2000 +0.33%) marginally outperformed. VIX rose 2.82% to 16.8, flagging residual unease. Accenture surged 20%+ on an earnings beat and strong AI-driven bookings guidance, lifting the broader IT-services group.

**Energy** — WTI crude +1.75% to $92.00, extending its run as US-Iran tensions escalate (fresh Treasury sanctions on Iran's auto and rail sectors) and Canada fast-tracks a new oil pipeline.

**Gold & Crypto** — Gold +0.34% to $4,200.80; Bitcoin +1.46% to $84,778, both gaining modestly as alternative stores of value amid dollar strength.

**Autos** — GM Q3 US sales fell 5.5%; Toyota gained share on EV/hybrid strength. Tesla European September registrations continued their recovery.
