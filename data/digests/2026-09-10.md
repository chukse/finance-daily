# Daily Market Brief — 2026-09-10

**TL;DR:** WTI crude cracking $100/bbl reignited inflation fears, sent 10-year yields to 4.91% (highest since 2023), and repriced Fed hike odds higher — dragging equities broadly lower.

---

## What Moved Markets

**Oil & rates dominated.** WTI surged 4.0% to nearly $100 ($99.88), driven by U.S.-Iran naval clashes near the Strait of Hormuz and escalating tensions around Pickaxe Mountain. That single shock cascaded through everything else.

**Treasuries sold off hard.** The 10-year yield jumped 8 bps to 4.91%, the highest print since late 2023. Markets are now pricing close to a coin-flip on a Fed hike at next week's meeting — a dramatic repricing from days ago.

**Equities fell broadly but not violently.** S&P 500 -0.41%, Dow -0.47%, Russell 2000 -0.58% (small caps most exposed to rate sensitivity). VIX popped 5.9% to 17.4 — elevated but not panicked.

**Housing deteriorated further.** August home sales fell to their lowest level of the year despite inventory hitting a 7-year high. Mortgage rates at their highest in over a year are the clear culprit.

**Dollar barely moved** (+0.14%), and gold slipped 0.35% — both suggesting the selloff is rate-driven rather than a pure flight-to-safety event.
