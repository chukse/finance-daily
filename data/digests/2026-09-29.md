# Daily Market Brief — 2026-09-29

**TL;DR:** A 5.276% 10-year Treasury yield — the dominant force in markets — dragged equities broadly lower, crushed gold, and pressured small-caps hardest.

---

## What moved markets

**Rates driving everything.** The 10-year yield rose another 3.6 bps to 5.276%, a level that continues to reprice risk assets. The WSJ explicitly flagged the "relentless rise in Treasury yields" as the session's driver. Small-caps bore the most pain (Russell 2000 -0.75%), consistent with higher rate sensitivity and tighter financing conditions for smaller firms.

**Gold cracked.** Down 3.25% to $4,181 — a sharp single-day move, likely a combination of the stronger dollar (+0.36% to 101.56 DXY) and forced de-risking/margin selling as yields spike.

**Oil fell on Saudi supply return.** WTI -2.21% to $90.55 after Saudi Arabia resumed Red Sea oil exports, easing supply concerns that had kept crude elevated.

**Consumer confidence missed badly.** Conference Board's September read fell to its lowest since 2014, citing elevated oil prices and inflation worries — adding a stagflationary undertone to the session.

**VIX ticked to 16.23** (+1.00%), elevated but not panicked — markets are stressed, not disorderly.
