# Daily Market Brief — 2026-09-15

**TL;DR:** The 10-year Treasury yield hit a 19-year high above 5%, hammering equities broadly and pushing mortgage rates to 7.17%.

---

## What Moved Markets

**Rates shock:** The 10-year yield crossed 5% intraday (closed +6.7 bps at 4.994%), a level not seen since 2007. That single move drove the day's narrative — equity selloff, housing fears, and dollar strength.

**Equities:** Broad-based decline. Dow -0.88% (~500+ points), Russell 2000 -0.92% led the downside; small caps most rate-sensitive. S&P -0.46%, Nasdaq -0.73%. VIX jumped 3.4% to 17.68 — elevated but not panicked.

**Oil surge:** WTI +3.26% to $104.70, adding stagflation anxiety on top of rate pressure. Combination of rising yields + rising energy costs is a dual headwind for risk assets.

**Gold and crypto sold off:** Gold -0.64% to $4,324 despite typical safe-haven logic — real yield pressure winning out. Bitcoin -2.28% to $76,384; Ethereum -3.45% to $2,428, the latter underperforming notably.

**Fed focus:** Wall Street parsing the upcoming dot plot for rate hike signals. Markets pricing the first hike in 3 years. Bessent defended the administration's economic record in a contentious Senate hearing while simultaneously defending 5% yields as manageable.
