# Daily Market Brief — 2026-07-08

**TL;DR:** Trump declared the Iran ceasefire "over," sending oil up 4.8% and triggering a broad risk-off rotation with tech leading declines.

---

## What Moved Markets

**The macro driver: Iran ceasefire collapse**
Trump's announcement that the interim Iran deal is finished dominated the session. WTI crude surged $3.37 to $73.82, a two-week high, as Strait of Hormuz traffic concerns resurfaced — Kalshi traders see disruptions lasting into 2027. The IMF separately warned the Iran conflict will leave an inflation scar through at least 2027.

**Equities sold off, tech hardest hit**
- Nasdaq −1.16%, S&P −0.45%, Dow −0.25%, Russell 2000 −0.90%
- VIX jumped 7.44% to 17.33 — elevated but not yet alarming
- 10Y yield ticked up 3 bps to 4.559%, adding rate pressure to growth names

**Gold counterintuitively fell**
Gold dropped 1.53% to $4,082. Likely dynamic: oil-driven inflation fears lifted real yields slightly, and some traders rotated into energy rather than defensive metals. Silver also slipped below $60.

**Dollar barely moved (+0.08%)** — geopolitical shock absorbed without a major flight-to-dollar bid, suggesting markets view this as regional rather than systemic.

**Trade escalation: Spain**
Trump ordered a halt to U.S. trade with Spain over NATO spending and Iran-related disagreements, adding a fresh transatlantic friction point.
