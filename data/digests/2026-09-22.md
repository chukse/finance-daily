# Daily Market Brief — 2026-09-22

**TL;DR:** Oil's sharpest single-day drop in months dominated the session as U.S.-Iran diplomatic signals lifted rate-sensitive and growth stocks, pushing the Nasdaq to a fresh all-time high while the Dow lagged on energy drag.

---

## What Moved Markets

**Oil (-4.64% to $91.34/bbl)** was the day's dominant force. Trump told the UN that Iran will reach a deal after the midterms, Saudi Arabia restarted its East-West pipeline, and Brent fell back below $100. Energy stocks pulled the Dow (-0.59%) lower while broader indices were insulated.

**Nasdaq (+0.27%) hit an intraday record high**, driven largely by Alphabet/Google gains and relief that easing Middle East tensions reduce the tail-risk premium. The Russell 2000 (+0.36%) also outperformed, consistent with a risk-on, lower-oil read.

**10Y yield at 4.96%** (-0.4bp), staying just below the psychologically significant 5% level. Fed's Williams said the rate-control toolkit is "working well" — no new hawkish signal. The VIX dropped 3.77% to 14.31, reflecting the day's calmer tone.

**Dollar (+0.17%)** and **gold (-0.26%)** were both quiet. Bitcoin (-0.29%) and ETH (-1.14%) drifted lower despite nearly $1B in Bitcoin ETF inflows reported.
